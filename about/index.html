About JEBN Engineering LLC.

JEBN prepares swimming pool drawing packages for US permits and construction: permit drawings, construction drawings, shop drawings and drafting support for pool builders, designers and engineers.

The company

JEBN Engineering LLC is a Florida limited liability company. It is on file with the Florida Division of Corporations under document number L26000581157. Benatic Paul Martin, the founder, is your contact for every project.

What JEBN does

  • Pool permit drawings. Site plan, pool plan, sections and details drawn to your permit office’s checklist.
  • Pool construction drawings. Coordinated plans, sections, construction details and equipment layouts.
  • Pool structural drawings. Shell sections, steel schedules and details drafted from your engineer’s design.
  • Plumbing & hydraulic drawings. Piping plans, schematics, equipment layouts and TDH calculation sheets.
  • Pool shop drawings. Tile, coping, gutter, steel and equipment details for fabrication and installation.
  • Outsourced pool drafting. Extra drafting capacity for builders and designers, drawn on your standards.
  • Drawings for your engineer of record. Drawing sets drafted to the design of the engineer who seals your project.
  • Landscape design drawings. Master plans, hardscape layouts and planting drawings.

Drawings are delivered as DWG and PDF files. Your quote lists the sheets, the revisions included and the delivery date before you commit. How the service works · Sample drawings

What JEBN does not do

JEBN is a drafting business, not a licensed engineering firm, and does not seal drawings. Where your permit office requires an engineer’s seal, a Professional Engineer licensed in your state must direct the engineering and seal the set. How the engineer’s seal works
