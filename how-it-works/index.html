FROM FIRST EMAIL TO DRAWING SET

Four steps to your drawing set.

  1. 1

    You send what you have

    Your city and state, a sketch or existing plans, the survey or site plan, and your permit office’s checklist or review comments. A sketch is enough to start.

  2. 2

    We reply with a quote

    We reply by email within 24 hours with questions or a quote. The quote lists every sheet, the revisions included and the delivery date. Asking is free.

  3. 3

    We draft the set

    Plans, sections, details and schedules are drawn to the checklist. Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after all project inputs are received.

  4. 4

    You receive the files

    A PDF set for your permit submission and the DWG files. You or your builder submit the set, and your permit office decides approval. If it sends comments, send them to us.

Who seals the drawings

JEBN prepares the drawings. JEBN is a drafting business, not a licensed engineering firm, and does not seal drawings. Where your permit office requires an engineer’s seal, a Professional Engineer licensed in your state must direct the engineering and seal the set. You can use your own engineer, or we can ask one licensed in your state to take your project. That engineer decides whether to take it and sets the fee for the engineering.

WHO DOES WHAT

Four parties, four jobs.

A pool permit set passes through several hands. This is who does what on a JEBN project.

Get my quote →
  • You or your pool builderSend the project information, choose the equipment and finishes, and submit the set to the permit office.
  • JEBNDrafts the drawing set and draws the changes within the revisions in your quote. JEBN does not seal drawings.
  • The engineer of recordNeeded where your state’s law or your permit office requires an engineer’s seal. Directs the engineering while the drawings are prepared, and seals the set.
  • Your permit officePublishes the checklist, reviews the set and decides approval.
