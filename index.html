QUESTIONS

Common pool engineering questions.

When does the two-business-day turnaround start?

Once we have received all project inputs. It covers preparation of residential pool drawings. PE review and sealing, where required, and the permit office’s review have separate timelines.

What should I send to get started?

Your project city and state, a site plan or sketch, pool dimensions if known, and the services you need. Include existing plans and any permit review comments if you have them.

Will I receive editable files?

Yes. You receive both DWG and PDF files for the agreed drawing package.

Do you work with pool contractors and builders?

Yes. We work with homeowners, pool contractors and builders on engineering plans, permit drawings, calculations and drawing updates within an agreed scope.

How are PE review and permit approval handled?

When your permit office requires a seal, a Professional Engineer licensed in the project state reviews the package, takes responsible charge and seals it. We confirm the responsible engineer before work begins. The permit office decides approval.

What if plan review comes back with comments?

Send us the comments. Your quote states the revision support included, and we identify the sheets and calculations each comment affects.

Do you know my city or county’s permit rules?

Each state page lists permit notes for named cities and counties, with links to the official sources. Send your permit office’s checklist with your request and we draw to it.

How is drafting for pool builders priced?

Per set or per sheet, agreed before work starts. Your quote lists the sheets, file formats and revision allowance.

Can you match our drawing standards?

Yes. Send a sample set and we adopt your layer naming, title block, sheet numbering and detail conventions.
