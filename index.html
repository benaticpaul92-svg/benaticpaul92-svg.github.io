Three steps to your drawing set.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Deliver

    You receive the PDF set for your permit submission and the DWG files. Your quote states the revisions included. Your permit office decides approval and timing.

Who seals the drawings

JEBN prepares the drawings. JEBN is a drafting business, not a licensed engineering firm, and does not seal drawings. Where your permit office requires an engineer’s seal, a Professional Engineer licensed in your state must direct the engineering and seal the set. You can use your own engineer, or we can ask one licensed in your state to take your project. That engineer decides whether to take it and sets the fee for the engineering. The permit office decides approval. If it sends comments, send them to us. Your quote states the revisions included.

QUESTIONS

Common questions about pool drawings.

When does the two-business-day turnaround start?

Once we have received all project inputs. It covers preparation of residential pool drawings. Where your permit needs an engineer’s seal, the engineer’s work takes additional time, and so does the permit office’s review.

What should I send to get started?

Your project city and state, a site plan or sketch, pool dimensions if known, and the drawings you need. Include existing plans and any permit review comments if you have them.

Will I receive editable files?

Yes. You receive both DWG and PDF files for the agreed drawing package.

Do you work with pool contractors and builders?

Yes. We work with homeowners, pool contractors and builders on permit drawings, construction drawings and drawing updates within an agreed scope.

Who seals the drawings?

JEBN prepares the drawings. JEBN is a drafting business, not a licensed engineering firm, and does not seal drawings. Where your permit office requires an engineer’s seal, a Professional Engineer licensed in your state must direct the engineering and seal the set. You can use your own engineer, or we can ask one licensed in your state to take your project. That engineer decides whether to take it and sets the fee for the engineering. The permit office decides approval.

What if plan review comes back with comments?

Send us the comments. Your quote states the revision support included, and we identify the sheets each comment affects. Comments on the engineering go to the engineer of record.

Do you know my city or county’s permit rules?

Each state page lists permit notes for named cities and counties, with links to the official sources. Send your permit office’s checklist with your request and we draw to it.

How is drafting for pool builders priced?

Per set or per sheet, agreed before work starts. Your quote lists the sheets, file formats and revision allowance.

Can you match our drawing standards?

Yes. Send a sample set and we adopt your layer naming, title block, sheet numbering and detail conventions.
