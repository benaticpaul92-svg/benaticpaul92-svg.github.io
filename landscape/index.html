Who We Are

About JEBN Landscape Studio

"We design outdoor spaces that belong to their climate — beautiful on day one, and still thriving years later."

JEBN Landscape Studio is a landscape design and architecture practice specialising in hardscape, softscape and planting design — producing architectural visuals and build-ready technical drawings for clients across the USA, UK, the Gulf and worldwide.

Climate-Adaptive by Default

We design for the climate that matters to your project — whether that's US hardiness zones and HOA-friendly layouts, or hot, arid conditions with their heat, salinity and water constraints. Every planting plan is grounded in what genuinely performs on site, not just what looks good in a render.

Visual & Technical, One Studio

We take projects from concept visuals all the way to build-ready construction drawings. Photorealistic renders win the client; precise technical packages get it built. Doing both under one roof keeps the design intent intact from first sketch to final paving stone.

Our Expertise

Martin, founder and principal designer, brings 10+ years' experience across private villas, residential developments and commercial projects — based in the UAE and delivering for clients in the USA, UK and beyond — combined with hardscape detailing, planting design and architectural visualisation.

We've built a reputation for:

  • Climate-adaptive planting and water-wise irrigation design
  • US hardiness zones and HOA-friendly layouts
  • Integrated hardscape and softscape master plans
  • Photorealistic architectural renders and 3D visuals
  • Build-ready technical and construction drawings
  • Remote delivery for clients across the USA, UK, the Gulf & worldwide

Whether you need a full landscape master plan, a set of presentation visuals, or technical drawings to hand a contractor, we're here to help — wherever your project is.

Questions

Frequently Asked

What exactly do I receive?

DWG and PDF drawing sets, planting plans and schedules, irrigation layouts, bills of quantities and photorealistic 3D renders — depending on the scope you choose.

Can you design for my climate?

Yes — planting is matched to your site, whether that's US hardiness zones and HOA-friendly layouts or hot, arid conditions. We design with species that genuinely perform where you build.

Do you work with overseas clients and time zones?

Yes — we deliver for clients across the USA, UK, Canada and the Gulf, working comfortably around your time zone with digital delivery.

How fast can you deliver?

It depends on scope — smaller residential plans can be delivered within days. We confirm the timeline with you before work starts.
