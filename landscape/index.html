BEFORE YOU ASK

Questions about landscape drawings.

What do I receive?

DWG and PDF drawing sets, planting plans and schedules, irrigation layouts, bills of quantities and 3D renders, depending on the scope you choose.

Can you design for my climate?

Yes. Planting is matched to your site, whether that means US hardiness zones and HOA-friendly layouts or hot, arid conditions. We choose species that perform where you build.

Do you work with clients in other time zones?

Yes. We deliver for clients across the USA, UK, Canada and the Gulf, and work around your time zone with digital delivery.

How fast can you deliver?

It depends on the scope. Smaller residential plans can be delivered within days. We confirm the timeline with you before work starts.

Can the drawings be sealed for a permit?

JEBN does not seal drawings. If your permit office asks for sealed landscape or site drawings, a landscape architect or engineer licensed in your state has to take responsibility for them and seal them. Send the office’s checklist with your request, so we can tell you which sheets it covers.

How do we start?

Send your scope through the form on this page. We reply within 24 hours with questions or a quote. No payment is needed to ask.
