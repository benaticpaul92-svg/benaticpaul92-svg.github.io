PERMIT SET OR CONSTRUCTION SET

Permit set or construction set?

A permit set shows the permit office what will be built. A construction set gives the crew the dimensions and details to build from. Many projects start with the permit set and add construction sheets once the permit is issued.

Ask about your project →
  • Permit setSite plan, pool plan, sections and the details the permit office’s checklist asks for.
  • Construction setLayout dimensions, steel, plumbing runs and finish details for the trades.
  • Shop drawingsFabrication details for tile, coping, gutters, steel and equipment.

BEFORE YOU ASK

Questions about pool construction drawings.

What is the difference between permit drawings and construction drawings?

Permit drawings show the permit office what will be built. Construction drawings add the dimensions, steel, plumbing and finish details the crew builds from. Many projects need both, and the two sets have to agree.

Can you draw from my builder’s sketch?

Yes. Send the sketch with dimensions and depths, the survey or site plan if you have one, and the equipment you plan to use. We tell you what is missing before drawing work starts.

Do construction drawings include the steel?

Yes, where it is part of your quote. Reinforcement is drawn from your engineer’s design or your builder’s standard details. JEBN drafts the steel details and does not design them.

Will I receive editable files?

Yes. You receive DWG and PDF files for the agreed drawing package.

Who seals the drawings?

JEBN prepares the drawings and does not seal them. Where your permit office requires an engineer’s seal, a Professional Engineer licensed in your state must direct the engineering and seal the set.

How fast can you prepare the construction drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. Where your permit needs an engineer’s seal, the engineer’s work takes additional time, and so does the permit office’s own review.
