Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This notice explains how JEBN Engineering Studio uses information provided through this website. For questions, contact Benatic Paul Martin at benatic@jebnengineering.com.

Project enquiries

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address and project description. Project location, company and timeline are optional. These details are used to respond to your request and discuss the work. Do not include payment-card details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information.

When online sending is connected, Formspree processes submissions and delivers notifications to our business contact inboxes. Its service may process technical information such as an IP address to operate the form and filter spam. You can read the Formspree privacy policy.

If you choose the email option, your email application handles the message. Opening a draft does not send it. You can also copy your message and send it using your preferred email service.

Website operation and measurement

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and font providers may receive technical information when your browser requests pages or fonts. See the GitHub privacy statement and Google privacy policy.

When present on the current page, campaign source, medium and campaign-name labels may be included with an enquiry so we can understand how the request reached us. Form entries are not placed in page URLs or sent to advertising analytics by the enquiry code. This version does not load Google Ads conversion tracking.

Advertising

Display advertising is not active in this version. If Google AdSense is enabled, this notice and the available privacy choices will be updated to match that setup.

When Google advertising is active, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on earlier visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalize ads. You can manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center and learn about participating providers at YourAdChoices.

Your information

You can contact us to ask about information you have submitted or to request a correction or deletion. Information may need to be kept for an ongoing project or applicable recordkeeping requirements. Contact benatic@jebnengineering.com with your request.
