Before we draw:
your pool project checklist.

Use this list to put your project brief together. You do not need every answer to start a conversation; we will identify any missing inputs before drawing work begins.

Download checklist (.txt) ↓

1. The project

  • Project city, state and permitting authority, if known.
  • Residential or commercial use; new pool or renovation.
  • Your role: owner, pool contractor, builder or designer.

2. The starting information

  • Site plan or survey showing the property and proposed pool location.
  • Sketch, dimensions, depths and preferred features.
  • Existing drawings and permit review comments, if available.
  • Equipment preferences and known site constraints.

3. The package you need

  • Engineering plans, permit drawings, structural details, hydraulics or shop drawings.
  • Engineering review or sealing requirements to confirm.
  • Your target date and project contact.

Ready to discuss it?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after all project inputs are received. PE review and authority approval timelines are separate.
