    You receive the PDF set for your permit submission and the DWG files. Your quote states the revisions included. Your permit office decides approval and timing.

Engineering & PE review

When your permit office requires a seal, a Professional Engineer licensed in the project state reviews the package, takes responsible charge and seals it. We confirm the responsible engineer before work begins. The permit office decides approval. If it sends comments, send them to us. Your quote states the revisions included.

Optional: 3D pool visualization

A 3D view can help you explore the proposed layout and materials. Ask to include it as a separate item alongside the drawing package.

BEFORE YOU ASK

Questions about outsourced drafting.

Whose name is on the drawings?

Yours. Drawings are delivered on your title block and standards, and your client relationship stays with you.

Can you match our drawing standards?

Yes. Send a sample set and we adopt your layer naming, title block, sheet numbering and detail conventions.

How fast do you return a set?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. For regular work we agree a delivery schedule with you.

How is the work priced?

Per set or per sheet, agreed before work starts. Your quote lists the sheets, file formats and revision allowance.

Can a set be sealed by an engineer?

Yes, where the project needs it. PE review and sealing can be added for that project, and we confirm the responsible engineer before work begins.
