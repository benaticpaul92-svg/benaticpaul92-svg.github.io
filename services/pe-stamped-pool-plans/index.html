The Permit Bottleneck

What sealing actually solves

That seal requirement is where a lot of contractors lose two or three weeks — chasing down a local engineer, waiting on their queue, then paying a premium for a review of drawings someone else already produced.

We close that gap. JEBN produces your complete drawing package, and a Professional Engineer licensed in the state of record reviews it, takes responsible charge, and applies their seal and signature. One deliverable, ready to submit.

Digital seals are provided where the jurisdiction accepts them, which is now most. Wet-sealed hard copies are available on request.

What You Receive
  • 01Structural plans — shell design, reinforcement schedules, soil-bearing assumptions and details to the governing code
  • 02Hydraulic and plumbing — pipe sizing, velocity compliance, TDH calculations, equipment and pump-room schematics
  • 03Electrical and bonding — equipotential bonding, GFCI and clearance details to NEC Article 680
  • 04Barrier and safety compliance — enclosure, alarm and entrapment provisions to the applicable state statute
  • 05Site and layout — setbacks, easements, elevations and drainage as required by your jurisdiction
Questions

Frequently Asked

Are your drawings actually sealed, or do I need to find my own engineer?

Sealed. A Professional Engineer licensed in your state reviews the package, takes responsible charge, and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set — you don't need to source a local engineer separately.

Which states can you seal in?

All 50, through licensed engineers in each state. Florida, Texas, Arizona and California carry dedicated code coverage given the volume of pool work in those markets.

Who is legally responsible for the sealed drawings?

The sealing engineer. They review the package, take responsible charge under their state licence, and carry professional liability for the sealed documents. JEBN produces the drawings and calculations that go into that review.

How long does sealing add to turnaround?

Typically two to three business days on top of drafting. Standard residential sets run about five business days end to end from approved markup.

What if plan review comes back with comments?

We address them and reissue the sealed set. Revisions arising from plan-review comments are included in the original scope.

Do you provide digital or wet seals?

Digital seals where the jurisdiction accepts them, which is now most. Wet-sealed hard copies are available on request.

Can you seal commercial and public pools?

Yes. Commercial work carries additional review — Chapter 31B and Title 22 in California, FAC 64E-9 in Florida, TDLR and DSHS in Texas. Send the project type and we'll confirm the path.
