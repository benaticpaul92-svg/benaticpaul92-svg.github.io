Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you produce the TDH worksheet some counties require?

Yes. Several Florida counties publish a simplified TDH worksheet as a required submittal. We complete it as part of the hydraulic package rather than leaving it for you to fill in.

What velocity limits do you design to?

Standard practice is 6 ft/s on suction and 8 ft/s on return lines, tightened where the local code or the pump manufacturer requires it.
