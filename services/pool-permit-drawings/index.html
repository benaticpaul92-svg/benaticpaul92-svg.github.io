Who seals the drawings

JEBN prepares the drawings and does not seal them. Where your permit office requires an engineer’s seal, a Professional Engineer licensed in your state must direct the engineering and seal the set. You can use your own engineer, or we can ask one licensed in your state to take your project. That engineer decides whether to take it and sets the fee for the engineering. The permit office decides approval. If it sends comments, send them to us. Your quote states the revisions included.

BEFORE YOU ASK

Questions about pool permit drawings.

What does a pool permit drawing set include?

Usually a site plan with setbacks and the barrier, a pool plan with dimensions and depths, sections, structural details, a plumbing schematic and an equipment schedule. Your permit office’s checklist decides the exact list, and your quote names every sheet.

Do pool permit drawings need an engineer’s stamp?

Your state’s law and your permit office’s rules decide. Some offices ask for plans sealed by a Professional Engineer licensed in the state. JEBN prepares the drawings and does not seal them. Where an engineer’s seal is required, a Professional Engineer licensed in your state must direct the engineering and seal the set.

Can you work from my builder’s sketch?

Yes. Send the sketch with dimensions and depths, the survey or site plan if you have one, and your permit office’s checklist. We tell you what is missing before drawing work starts.

What if plan review comes back with comments?

Send us the comments. Your quote states the revisions included, and we identify the sheets each comment affects. Comments on the engineering go to the engineer of record.

Do you know my city or county’s permit rules?

Each state page lists permit notes for named cities and counties, with links to the official sources. Send your permit office’s checklist with your request and we draw to it.

How fast can you prepare the permit drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. Where your permit needs an engineer’s seal, the engineer’s work takes additional time, and so does the permit office’s own review.
