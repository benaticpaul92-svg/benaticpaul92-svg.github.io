FROM QUOTE TO DELIVERY

Three steps to your drawing set.

  1. 1

    Confirm the scope

    We review your request and reply by email within 24 hours with questions or a scoped quote. We identify missing inputs and what your permit office asks for.

  2. 2

    Prepare the drawings

    Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after all project inputs are received. Where your permit needs an engineer’s seal, the engineer’s work takes additional time.

  3. 3

    Deliver

    You receive the PDF set for your permit submission and the DWG files. Your quote states the revisions included. Your permit office decides approval and timing.

Who seals the drawings

JEBN prepares the drawings and does not seal them. Where your permit office requires an engineer’s seal, a Professional Engineer licensed in your state must direct the engineering and seal the set. You can use your own engineer, or we can ask one licensed in your state to take your project. That engineer decides whether to take it and sets the fee for the engineering. The permit office decides approval. If it sends comments, send them to us. Your quote states the revisions included.

BEFORE YOU ASK

Questions about pool shop drawings.

Do you deliver DWG as well as PDF?

Yes. You receive DWG and PDF files for the agreed sheets, so fabricators and your crew can work from the native files.

Do shop drawings need a PE seal?

Shop drawings are fabrication and installation documents. Ask your permit office or your client whether they need a seal. If they do, a Professional Engineer licensed in your state must direct the engineering and seal them. JEBN does not seal drawings.

Can you work from another designer’s plans?

Yes. Send the design or permit set and we draw the fabrication details that follow it. We tell you if something in the set conflicts with the detail being drawn.

How is a shop drawing package priced?

By the agreed sheet list. Your quote names the sheets, file formats, revisions included and delivery date before work starts.
