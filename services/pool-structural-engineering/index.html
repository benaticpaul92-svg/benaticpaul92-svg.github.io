BEFORE YOU ASK

Questions about pool structural plans.

Does my pool need structural engineering?

Your permit office decides. Many ask for engineered structural plans when the pool is close to a house footing, a retaining wall or a slope, when the ground is difficult, or when the shell has raised walls or a vanishing edge. Send the checklist and we identify what applies.

Do you design for expansive or poor soils?

Where a geotechnical report is available, the design follows its recommendations. Where one is not, the drawings state the assumed bearing capacity so the reviewing engineer and the plan reviewer can see what the design relies on.

Can you detail vanishing-edge and perimeter-overflow pools?

Yes. The structural scope covers the weir wall and catch basin, and the hydraulic design for the edge can be included in the same package.

Who seals the structural plans?

Where sealing is part of your agreed package, a Professional Engineer licensed in the project state takes responsible charge and seals the plans. We confirm the responsible engineer before work begins.

How fast can you prepare the structural drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.
