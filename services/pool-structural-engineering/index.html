Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you design for expansive or poor soils?

Yes. Where a geotechnical report is available we design to its recommendations. Where one is not, we state the assumed bearing capacity explicitly on the drawings so plan review and your sealing engineer can see exactly what the design relies on.

Can you handle vanishing-edge and perimeter-overflow pools?

Yes — these are a regular part of our work, including catch-basin sizing, weir-wall structural detail and the hydraulic design that goes with them.
