ALABAMA · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Alabama.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Birmingham

City of Birmingham Planning, Engineering & Permits, Permitting & Inspection DivisionOnline portal: Online Permit Center (Accela Citizen Access)

  • The Permitting & Inspection Division takes permit applications through the Online Permit Center, where applicants upload documents and track the application.
  • The city offers an interactive Permit Guide that asks questions to work out which permits a project needs.
  • The city's building codes page lists a 2024 Technical Code effective 10/1/2024 and the earlier Technical Code under Ordinance 15-164.

Source: Online Permit Center Introduction · City of Birmingham Permitting · Building Codes

Huntsville / Madison County

City of Huntsville Inspection DepartmentOnline portal: ePlans Review

  • The city's residential permit page says plans are submitted online through ePlans Review as PDF files, and approved permits are picked up in person.
  • The city lists the 2018 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code, 2018 International Residential Code and 2017 NEC among its adopted codes.
  • Projects in a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area must be submitted in person, and most building permits need a Flood Development Permit first.
  • For commercial pools, the city says Madison County Health Department approval and City Engineering sign-off on the site drawing are required.

Source: Residential permits - City of Huntsville · Commercial Building Inspection Requirements - City of Huntsville · Commercial Construction Permitting Process - City of Huntsville

Mobile

Build Mobile, Permitting and PredevelopmentOnline portal: CSS Online Permitting (Tyler EnerGov self-service)

  • Build Mobile's permitting page says its Permitting Department handles permits for swimming pools, fences and other construction types.
  • Permits are managed through the online self-service portal, and Build Mobile is on the third floor of the South Tower at 205 Government Street.
  • Build Mobile lists adopted 2021 IBC and 2021 IRC editions with local amendments, effective January 7, 2023, plus the 2020 NEC.
  • Build Mobile publishes a Residential Plan Review Checklist and a Residential Project Plan Review Application.

Source: Permitting - Build Mobile · Forms, Applications and Codes - Build Mobile · Permitting - City of Mobile

Alabama state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
Alabama Residential Building Code based on the 2015 IRC with state amendments (effective Oct 1, 2016), enforced only where cities and counties run permit programs; code adoption authority moved to the Home Builders Licensure Board in 2025.
Pool barriers
No statewide pool barrier statute; barrier requirements are set by the locally enforced residential code and local ordinance.
Contractor licensing
Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors licenses swimming pool construction of $5,000 or more (Ala. Code 34-8-1); the Home Builders Licensure Board licenses residential home builders above $10,000.
Engineer licensing
Alabama Board of Licensure for Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors

ALABAMA QUESTIONS

Questions about Alabama pool plans.

Is a state license needed to build a residential pool in Alabama?

Yes, when the pool costs $5,000 or more. The Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors treats swimming pool construction at that value as general contracting. Local building permits and inspections still apply.

Which residential code do Alabama pool permits follow?

The state Residential Building Code is based on the 2015 IRC, but it is enforced only where a city or county runs a permit program, and some cities, such as Mobile, enforce a newer locally adopted IRC. We confirm the edition in force for your jurisdiction before drawings are issued.

Are your Alabama pool plans sealed by a Alabama-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Alabama reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Alabama jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Alabama pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Alabama?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
