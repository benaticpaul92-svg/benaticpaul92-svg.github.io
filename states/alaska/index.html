ALASKA · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Alaska.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Anchorage (Municipality of Anchorage)

Municipality of Anchorage Development Services, Permit ManagementOnline portal: Dynamic Portal

  • Development Services handles permits and inspections, and applicants use the Dynamic Portal at permitcenter.muni.org.
  • Permit status and inspections can be tracked through a separate Check Permit Status page at bsd.muni.org.

Source: Municipality of Anchorage Development Services - Policies · Municipality of Anchorage Development Services - Permits

Fairbanks / Fairbanks North Star Borough

City of Fairbanks Building Department and Fairbanks North Star Borough Community Planning

  • The City of Fairbanks page lists no online permit portal; the building permit application is completed and signed, then submitted with two sets of plans.
  • Plans must include the items on the city's Residential Plan Submittal Checklist, and the applicant names an energy compliance method.
  • A zoning permit from the Fairbanks North Star Borough Planning Department must be included with the city building permit application.
  • The city reviews plans against the codes and local amendments it has adopted; the page does not name an edition.
  • The borough's Community Planning office offers a zoning permit that checks setbacks and use for the zoning district; applications go to planning@fnsb.gov.

Source: City of Fairbanks - How to Obtain a Permit · Fairbanks North Star Borough - Community Planning

Juneau (City and Borough of Juneau)

CBJ Community Development Department, Building and Permit CenterOnline portal: CBJ Energov self-service portal (Civic Access)

  • CBJ Building reviews permit applications and inspects construction against the International Building Code, with Juneau's local changes in Title 19.
  • Building, land use, engineering and fire permits can all be applied for at the Permit Center, and applications are filed through the online self-service portal.
  • Residential new construction applications need a to-scale site plan showing dimensions and property lines, per the city's residential new construction page.
  • The city's fence page requires a building permit for fences over seven feet tall, with a to-scale site plan and an engineer-designed structural plan; that page does not mention pools.

Source: CBJ Community Development - What We Do · CBJ Community Development - Residential New Construction · CBJ Community Development - Fences

Mat-Su Borough (outside Palmer, Wasilla and Houston)

Matanuska-Susitna Borough Permit Center, Development ServicesOnline portal: MSB Permit Portal

  • Building permit applications are submitted online through the borough's permit portal.
  • The borough has adopted the 2021 IBC, IFC, IMC and IEBC under Title 13 of the Alaska Administrative Code, effective October 28, 2022.
  • The borough page says the State of Alaska and the borough have not adopted the International Residential Code; the 2021 IRC is listed for reference only.
  • A Mandatory Land Use Permit is required outside Palmer, Wasilla and Houston for any structure within 75 feet of a watercourse or waterbody, with a site plan showing setbacks and nearby waterbodies.

Source: MSB - Plan Review and Permitting · MSB - Building Codes, 2021 Code Cycle · MSB - Mandatory Land Use Permit

Alaska state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
No mandatory statewide residential code; municipalities such as Anchorage, Fairbanks and Juneau adopt their own IRC editions, and AHFC-financed homes must meet the state BEES energy standard.
Pool barriers
Set by local ordinance in municipalities that adopt and enforce building codes; no state pool barrier statute.
Contractor licensing
Construction contractors register with the Alaska Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing; general contractors doing residential work need a Residential Contractor Endorsement, with no pool-specific license class.
Public pools
Alaska Department of Environmental Conservation regulates public pools and spas under 18 AAC 30, Article 5 (18 AAC 30.500 to 30.590) statewide except the Municipality of Anchorage, which runs its own program.
Engineer licensing
Alaska State Board of Registration for Architects, Engineers, and Land Surveyors (AELS)

ALASKA QUESTIONS

Questions about Alaska pool plans.

Does Alaska have a statewide code for residential pools?

No. The IRC is not adopted statewide for homes. Anchorage, Fairbanks, Juneau and other municipalities set their own codes and pool barrier rules, so drawings follow the adopted edition of the permitting municipality.

Who approves a public or commercial pool in Alaska?

Alaska DEC regulates public pools and spas under 18 AAC 30, Article 5, everywhere except the Municipality of Anchorage, which runs its own pool and spa plan review through its health department.

Are your Alaska pool plans sealed by a Alaska-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Alaska reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Alaska jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Alaska pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Alaska?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
