ARKANSAS · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Arkansas.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Little Rock

City of Little Rock Planning and Development, Building Codes DivisionOnline portal: Interactive Building-Construction Permit Program

  • The Building Codes Division issues construction permits and provides plan review and inspections for building, plumbing, mechanical and electrical work in the city.
  • The city's list of enforced construction codes, dated January 7, 2023, includes the 2021 International Swimming Pool & Spa Code with Appendix A.
  • The same list gives the 2021 AR Fire Prevention Code Vol. 3 as the residential code and the 2020 National Electrical Code with the city's Chapter 8 revisions.
  • The Civil Engineering Division page lists dechlorinated swimming pool discharges among allowable non-stormwater discharges to the municipal storm sewer system, citing Municipal Code Sec. 29.45.

Source: Planning and Development Divisions - City of Little Rock · City of Little Rock Enforced Construction Codes, January 7, 2023 (PDF) · Civil Engineering Division - City of Little Rock

Fayetteville

City of Fayetteville Development Services, Building Safety DivisionOnline portal: Civic Access Portal

  • The pool checklist asks for a scaled site plan with a north arrow, property lines, setbacks and easements, plus the pool's dimensions and distances from property lines and structures.
  • Plans must carry notes that the pool will comply with the barrier requirements of the 2021 ISPSC, Section 305, and that electrical work needs a separate electrical permit.
  • The checklist covers in-ground and above-ground pools and spas, and also asks for a signed Owner's Affidavit or a contractor's license.
  • Building Safety's current codes list includes the 2021 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code, the 2021 Arkansas Fire Prevention Code and the 2026 National Electrical Code.

Source: Pool S.D.P. Checklist (PDF) - City of Fayetteville Building Safety · Current Codes - City of Fayetteville · About the Civic Access Portal - City of Fayetteville

Bentonville

Bentonville Fire Department, Building & Fire Safety Division (Building Inspection)Online portal: eTrakit

  • The Building & Fire Safety Division of the Bentonville Fire Department issues pool permits, and applications go through the eTrakit online portal with attachments as PDF files.
  • The pool checklist asks for a scaled site plan showing the pool's distances to structures, setbacks and property lines, fences and gates, easements and any retaining walls.
  • The site plan must also show pool shell conductivity and bonding connections per NFPA 70, and a Rainfall Mitigation Worksheet is required for pools.
  • A fence used as the pool enclosure must be at least 48 inches high, with gates that swing away from the pool and are self-closing and self-latching.
  • Codes listed as enforced include the 2021 Arkansas Fire Prevention Code Vol III (Residential) and the 2020 National Electrical Code.

Source: Swimming Pool Permit Submittal Checklist (PDF) - City of Bentonville · Residential Applications - City of Bentonville · Building Inspections - Building & Fire Safety - City of Bentonville · Building Codes - City of Bentonville

Rogers

Rogers Fire Department, Community Risk Reduction DivisionOnline portal: Online Permitting Portal (Cityworks)

  • Swimming pools are on the city's list of work that needs a permit; the Fire Department's Community Risk Reduction Division reviews residential plans before permits are issued.
  • The pool contractor, or a homeowner building their own pool, applies on the online portal with a site plan, pool and equipment layout and structural diagram of the pool.
  • A master electrician must pull the electric permit and do the pool and deck bonding and grounding; adding gas for a pool heater needs a master plumber's plumbing permit.
  • Adopted codes include the 2021 International Pool & Spa Code, the 2021 Arkansas Fire Prevention Code Vol. III - Residential and the 2020 National Electrical Code (NEC).
  • The city's listed design requirements include an 18 in. frost line depth and Seismic Design Category B.

Source: Residential Projects - City of Rogers · FAQ: When is a permit required? - City of Rogers · Adopted Codes - City of Rogers · Community Risk Reduction Division - Fire Department - City of Rogers · Online Permitting Portal - City of Rogers

Arkansas state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2021 Arkansas Fire Prevention Code, Volume III (2021 IRC as amended by the State Fire Marshal), effective Jan 1, 2023 and applicable statewide; local jurisdictions may adopt only this edition.
Pool barriers
No statewide pool barrier statute; barrier requirements are set by local ordinance and the locally enforced residential code.
Contractor licensing
Arkansas Contractors Licensing Board (Department of Labor and Licensing): Home Improvement license with the Swimming Pools, Spas specialty for residential work over $2,000; commercial license for non-single-family projects of $50,000 or more.
Public pools
Arkansas Department of Health, Division of Environmental Health Protection, reviews public and semi-public pool plans under its Rules Pertaining to Swimming, Spray and Wading Pools.
Engineer licensing
Arkansas State Board of Licensure for Professional Engineers and Professional Surveyors

ARKANSAS QUESTIONS

Questions about Arkansas pool plans.

Which license does a residential pool builder need in Arkansas?

For residential pool work over $2,000, a Home Improvement license with the Swimming Pools, Spas specialty through the Arkansas Contractors Licensing Board. Pools on commercial projects of $50,000 or more need a commercial license.

Do public pools in Arkansas need state plan review?

Yes. Public and semi-public pools are reviewed by the Arkansas Department of Health under its swimming pool rules. ADH recommends that plans be prepared by a professional engineer registered in Arkansas.

Are your Arkansas pool plans sealed by a Arkansas-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Arkansas reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Arkansas jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Arkansas pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Arkansas?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
