COLORADO · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Colorado.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Denver

Denver Community Planning and DevelopmentOnline portal: Denver e-permits

  • A permanent pool needs zoning, building and sewer use and drainage (SUDP) permits, applied for through the city's online e-permits system.
  • The site plan must be to scale with a north arrow and show lot lines, structures, pool dimensions, distances to lot lines, and fences or barriers.
  • A boundary survey is required if the pool or any equipment is within 3 feet of the property line.
  • Electrical, plumbing or mechanical permits are separate quick permits when the pool adds lighting, a heater or piping.

Source: Swimming Pools, Spas, and Water Features - Denver

Lakewood

City of Lakewood BuildingOnline portal: eTRAKiT

  • A building permit is required for any pool, hot tub or spa on a residential property.
  • Lakewood says pools must meet its local amendments to the 2021 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code, 2021 International Plumbing Code and 2021 International Residential Code.
  • The application and plans are uploaded through eTRAKiT; the plot plan must be at 1 inch = 30 feet or a larger scale.
  • Properties with a pool need a lockable cover or a fenced yard; the handout sets the fence at a minimum of 48 inches high.

Source: 2023 Residential Pool and Hot Tub Requirements - Lakewood

Fort Collins

Fort Collins Building Services

  • The pool, pond or in-ground hot tub permit needs a building permit application, a scaled site plan and either manufacturer specifications or custom design details.
  • In-ground designs must include engineered foundation details, and plans must show safety barrier or cover details.
  • The page directs applicants to email the application to buildingservices@fortcollins.gov and does not name a portal for pool permits.

Source: Building Permits - Fort Collins

Aurora

City of Aurora Building Division

  • Aurora lists the 2021 International Residential Code and the 2021 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code among its adopted codes.
  • The city's Building Division page says permits are applied for online.

Source: Adopted Building Codes - City of Aurora · Permits - City of Aurora

Colorado state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
No statewide residential code; Colorado is a home-rule state where cities and counties adopt their own IRC editions, subject to state minimum energy code requirements when they update codes.
Pool barriers
Set by local ordinance through each jurisdiction's adopted IRC or ISPSC; no state pool barrier statute.
Contractor licensing
No statewide license for general or pool contractors; contractors are licensed locally (for example Denver or the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department), while plumbers and electricians hold state licenses.
Public pools
Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment, Swimming Pools and Mineral Baths regulation, 5 CCR 1003-5 (effective Dec 15, 2020); C.R.S. 25-1-508 lets certain local agencies apply the MAHC.
Engineer licensing
Colorado State Board of Licensure for Architects, Professional Engineers and Professional Land Surveyors

COLORADO QUESTIONS

Questions about Colorado pool plans.

Is there a state pool contractor license in Colorado?

No. Colorado does not license general or pool contractors at state level. Cities and counties, such as Denver and the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department, license contractors and issue pool permits under their own adopted codes.

Why do Colorado pool drawings usually need a soils report?

Front Range claystone and bentonitic clays can swell and heave pool shells and decks. A site-specific geotechnical report sets the basis for overexcavation, subdrainage and deck isolation in the structural design.

Are your Colorado pool plans sealed by a Colorado-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Colorado reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Colorado jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Colorado pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Colorado?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
