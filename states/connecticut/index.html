CONNECTICUT · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Connecticut.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

West Hartford (Hartford County)

West Hartford Building DepartmentOnline portal: CityView Permit Online Portal

  • The town requires all permit applications, including building permits, to go through its CityView online portal; paper applications are no longer accepted.
  • The town's Building FAQ says it follows the State Building Code, which includes the 2021 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code.
  • Depending on the project, approval may also be needed from Planning/Zoning, Engineering, Health or the Fire Marshal.

Source: Building Permits - West Hartford · Building FAQs - West Hartford

Norwalk (Fairfield County)

Norwalk Planning and Zoning

  • Swimming pools are treated as accessory structures that need a Zoning Permit first, then a Building Permit after zoning approval.
  • The application needs a site plan showing the pool's location relative to property lines at a legible scale, plus dimensionally accurate plans of current and proposed conditions.
  • Pools in a flood zone need extra documentation, and a flood-zone application may apply.
  • The page asks for three paper copies of plans, delivered in person or by mail; no online portal is named.

Source: Apply for a Zoning Permit - Norwalk

Darien (Fairfield County)

Darien Building DepartmentOnline portal: ViewPoint Online Permitting System

  • The town says all permit applications are submitted electronically through its ViewPoint online permitting system.
  • Its Work Requiring a Permit page says a permit is needed to install a pool or spa, in-ground or above ground.
  • Its online-permitting document list includes a Pool Water Alarm Affidavit and a Swimming Pool Fence Agreement.

Source: Building Department - Darien · Work Requiring a Permit - Darien · Frequently Used Documents for Online Permitting - Darien

Shelton (Fairfield County)

Shelton Building Department

  • The city's building permit procedure covers all construction and lists swimming pools among the permits the Building Official issues.
  • Listed steps include site plan approval through Planning and Zoning and Inland Wetlands where applicable, a zoning compliance application, and a copy of the contractor's licence and insurance certificate.

Source: Building Department - Shelton

Connecticut state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2022 Connecticut State Building Code (2021 IRC and 2021 ISPSC with state amendments), mandatory statewide since Oct 1, 2022; a 2026 code based on the 2024 I-Codes awaits final approval.
Pool barriers
Residential barriers follow the 2021 ISPSC as amended in the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code.
Contractor licensing
Department of Consumer Protection issues the Swimming Pool Builder (SPB) license for new inground pools and spas, which also requires home improvement contractor registration; SP-1 and SP-2 cover pool service work.
Public pools
Connecticut Department of Public Health, Public Health Code, Regulations of Connecticut State Agencies Section 19-13-B33b (public swimming pools), with the DPH Public Swimming Pool Design Guide.
Engineer licensing
Connecticut State Board of Examiners for Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors

CONNECTICUT QUESTIONS

Questions about Connecticut pool plans.

What license does an inground pool builder need in Connecticut?

A Swimming Pool Builder (SPB) license from the Department of Consumer Protection, which also requires home improvement contractor registration. Service and repair work on pool equipment falls under the separate SP-1 and SP-2 licenses.

Which code applies to residential pools in Connecticut?

The 2022 Connecticut State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 IRC and 2021 ISPSC with state amendments. A 2026 code based on the 2024 ISPSC is pending; we design to the edition in force at permit submission.

Are your Connecticut pool plans sealed by a Connecticut-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Connecticut reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Connecticut jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Connecticut pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Connecticut?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
