FLORIDA · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Florida.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Miami-Dade County

Miami-Dade County Permitting and Inspection CenterOnline portal: Permit Submission Portal

  • An in-ground pool permit application is filed under Category 55 and lists the pool's total square footage, along with a contact sheet.
  • Design drawings must show the site plan, pool dimensions, electrical and plumbing connections and calculations, and must be signed and sealed by an architect or engineer.
  • Electrical and plumbing or gas subsidiary permits are required after the pool permit is issued and must cite the pool permit number.
  • Owner-builder pool permits need an additional form.

Source: Swimming Pool Permits - Miami-Dade County

Palm Beach County

Palm Beach County Planning, Zoning & Building, Building DivisionOnline portal: ePZB Admin Portal

  • Every pool or spa is issued as a primary permit, with sub-permits for the barrier, electric and any other trade.
  • An Affidavit of Understanding, signed by owner and contractor to confirm a 360-degree barrier, must be submitted with the pool application.
  • If a neighbor's wall or fence is used as part of the barrier, a Pool Barrier Agreement must be recorded in the Official Records of Palm Beach County and submitted with the application.

Source: Pool Barrier Requirements/Process PPM# PB-O-104 · Building Division - Palm Beach County PZB

Tampa (Hillsborough County)

City of Tampa Construction Services DivisionOnline portal: Accela Citizens Access

  • A residential pool submittal includes a site plan, structural plans, and plumbing piping and water supply calculations; the APSP-15 information sheet may be used for the calculations.
  • Structural plans must be signed and sealed, and engineered pool structural plans can be placed on file through a separate process.
  • The site plan must show easements, trees within 20 feet of the work, and a property-line-to-property-line cross section.
  • A signed and sealed topographic survey is required if the deck exceeds 1,000 square feet.

Source: Residential Pool and Spa - Plan Review (City of Tampa)

Orange County (unincorporated)

Orange County Division of Building SafetyOnline portal: Fast Track Online Services

  • In-ground pool construction plans must be prepared by a Florida-licensed Professional Engineer or Registered Architect.
  • Submittals include a site plan or survey, a lot grading and drainage plan with finished floor elevation, and ANSI/APSP 7 (TDH) and ANSI/APSP 15 worksheets or equivalent plan information.
  • A site-specific Swimming Pool Safety Affirmation is required, and fiberglass pools need hydrostatic pressure engineering and backfill methods.
  • The county cites the current Residential Florida Building Code, including Section 4501.17 for pool barriers.

Source: Residential Swimming Pool Permit - Orange County

Orlando

City of Orlando Building & Development, Permits & Inspections

  • The pool checklist asks for a topographic survey, a dimensioned site plan and a grading and drainage plan, plus floodplain items if the lot is in the 1% annual chance flood zone.
  • The topographic survey must be sealed by a Florida-licensed Professional Surveyor, and plans involving any re-grading must be sealed by a Florida-registered Professional Engineer or Architect.
  • A permitted fence is required for a pool 2 feet or more deep with an area of 36 square feet or more, generally 4 to 6 feet tall.
  • The checklist lists the building permit, electrical permit, pool safety act form, plan submission and a recorded Notice of Commencement as the application steps.

Source: Residential Swimming Pool and Spa Checklist - City of Orlando

Jacksonville (Duval County)

City of Jacksonville Public Works, Building Inspection DivisionOnline portal: JaxEPICS

  • The division publishes a Swimming Pool/SPA 1 & 2 Family checklist and a Private Swimming Pool Safety Affirmation form.
  • Permit applications are submitted online through JaxEPICS, which is also used to request inspections.
  • Most jobs over $5,000 need a Notice of Commencement, and the permit and notice must be posted on site before any inspection.

Source: Checklists, Forms and Documents - Jacksonville Building Inspection Division · FAQs - Jacksonville Building Inspection Division

Pinellas County (unincorporated and listed cities)

Pinellas County Building and Development Review Services DepartmentOnline portal: Pinellas County Access Portal

  • The county handles permits for unincorporated Pinellas and for Belleair Beach, Belleair Shore, Indian Rocks Beach, Kenneth City, Safety Harbor and Oldsmar.
  • Pools and spas are a permit type with separate in-ground and above-ground subtypes, all applied for through the Access Portal.
  • A Safety Act Letter is required for pool permits, and pools also need a no-objection letter from the electric company.
  • All structural work requires building plans digitally signed and sealed by an architect or engineer.

Source: Residential Permits - Pinellas County · Permitting Guide - Pinellas County

Statewide

  • Florida Statute 515.29 requires a residential pool barrier to be at least 4 feet high on the outside, with options for a perimeter barrier or a house wall with no door or window opening to the pool. Source: Florida Statutes s. 515.29

FLORIDA QUESTIONS

Questions about Florida pool plans.

Do you handle High-Velocity Hurricane Zone projects?

Yes. HVHZ work in Miami-Dade and Broward carries stricter structural and product-approval requirements, and we design to them rather than treating them as a standard Florida job.

Can you seal for public and commercial pools in Florida?

Yes. Public pools run through FAC 64E-9 construction plan approval, which is a different review path from residential. Send the project type and we'll confirm the path before you commit.

How fast can you prepare Florida pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Florida?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
