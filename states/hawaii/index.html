HAWAII · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Hawaii.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Honolulu (City and County of Honolulu, Oahu)

Department of Planning and Permitting (DPP)Online portal: HNL Build (permit application) and ePlans (plan upload)

  • Applicants apply for the building permit online through HNL Build, then upload plans to DPP through ePlans using a link emailed after the application.
  • The DPP requirements page says plans must be stamped and signed by a licensed professional when work is in a special management area, flood hazard area or special design district.
  • The same page also requires a licensed professional's stamp when principal structural members are reinforced concrete or structural steel.
  • Any project that does not qualify for an Instant Online Permit needs plans that meet DPP's Building Permit Plan Format Checklist.

Source: Building Permit Requirements - Department of Planning and Permitting, City and County of Honolulu

Kauai County

Department of Public Works, Building DivisionOnline portal: Electronic Plan Review (ePlan) and Building Permits Online

  • The County's building permit application form lists Swimming Pool as a type of work.
  • The application form says the separate zoning permit application is submitted through the County's connect.kauai.gov site, which requires an account.
  • Plans are submitted electronically through the Electronic Plan Review system after the application request is accepted and an email invitation is sent.
  • The Building Division's single-family plan review checklist says plans must comply with the 2018 IRC and 2018 IECC as adopted by the State of Hawaii, and the 2018 UPC and 2017 NEC as adopted by the County.

Source: Building Permit Guide - Kauai County Building Division · Electronic Plan Review - Kauai County Building Division · County of Kauai Department of Public Works Building Division - Building Permit Application · DPW/Building Division SFR/Duplex Plan Review Checklist 08/08/2023

Maui County

Department of Public Works, Development Services AdministrationOnline portal: MAPPS (Maui's Automated Planning and Permitting System)

  • The County's MAPPS site lets applicants apply for permits and plans online and use a Customer Self Service portal to check status and request inspections.

Source: Maui County MAPPS

Hawaii state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
Hawaii State Building Code based on the 2018 IRC, adopted by each county with local amendments; State Building Code Council updates were suspended by the Governor's housing emergency proclamation in 2025.
Pool barriers
Set by county building codes and ordinances (Honolulu, Hawaii, Maui and Kauai counties); there is no statewide pool barrier statute.
Contractor licensing
Hawaii Contractors License Board (DCCA) issues the C-49 Swimming Pool Contractor specialty license for pools, hot tubs, pool decks and related plumbing and equipment.
Public pools
Hawaii Department of Health, HAR Title 11, Chapter 10 Public Swimming Pools; HAR 11-10-3 requires plans by a Hawaii-licensed architect or engineer and DOH approval before construction.
Engineer licensing
Hawaii Board of Professional Engineers, Architects, Surveyors and Landscape Architects

HAWAII QUESTIONS

Questions about Hawaii pool plans.

What contractor license is needed to build pools in Hawaii?

The C-49 Swimming Pool Contractor specialty license issued through the Hawaii Contractors License Board. It covers concrete, gunite, metal and plastic pools, hot tubs, pool decks and related plumbing and equipment.

Must Hawaii public pool plans be prepared by a licensed professional?

Yes. HAR 11-10-3 requires public pool plans to be prepared by or under a Hawaii-licensed architect or engineer and approved by the Department of Health before construction, with a plan review fee.

Are your Hawaii pool plans sealed by a Hawaii-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Hawaii reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Hawaii jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Hawaii pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Hawaii?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
