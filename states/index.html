FROM ENQUIRY TO DELIVERY

A clear scope.
A clear next step.

  1. 1. Confirm the scope

    We review your enquiry and reply with questions or a scoped quote. We identify missing inputs and the project’s review requirements.

  2. 2. Prepare the drawings

    Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after all project inputs are received. PE review and sealing have a separate timeline.

  3. 3. Review & deliver

    Receive the agreed DWG and PDF package. We confirm revision support in your quote. The local authority decides permit approval and timing.
