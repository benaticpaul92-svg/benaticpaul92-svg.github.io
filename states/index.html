What a pool permit

Typical sheets in a residential permit set

  • Cover sheet with general notes
  • Site plan with setbacks and barrier
  • Pool plan with dimensions and depths
  • Long section and cross sections
  • Reinforcement and structural details
  • Steps, benches and raised features
  • Hydraulic schematic and pipe sizes
  • TDH calculation sheet and equipment schedule

Your quote lists the sheets for your project.

FROM QUOTE TO DELIVERY

Three steps to your drawing set.

  1. 1

    Confirm the scope

    We review your request and reply by email within 24 hours with questions or a scoped quote. We identify missing inputs and what your permit office asks for.

  2. 2

    Prepare the drawings

    Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after all project inputs are received. Where your permit needs an engineer’s seal, the engineer’s work takes additional time.

  3. 3

    Deliver

    You receive the PDF set for your permit submission and the DWG files. Your quote states the revisions included. Your permit office decides approval and timing.

Who seals the drawings

JEBN prepares the drawings. JEBN is a drafting business, not a licensed engineering firm, and does not seal drawings. Where your permit office requires an engineer’s seal, a Professional Engineer licensed in your state must direct the engineering and seal the set. You can use your own engineer, or we can ask one licensed in your state to take your project. That engineer decides whether to take it and sets the fee for the engineering. The permit office decides approval. If it sends comments, send them to us. Your quote states the revisions included.
