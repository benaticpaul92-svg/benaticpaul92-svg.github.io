INDIANA · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Indiana.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Carmel

Building and Code Services, Community Services DepartmentOnline portal: Online Permitting, with plan upload through Project Dox

  • Carmel applicants file a residential permit online, then get an email to upload plans to the Project Dox submittal system once a permit number is assigned.
  • The city says residential projects must meet the 2020 Indiana Residential Code, which is the 2018 IRC with the 2020 Indiana amendments.
  • Carmel's pool inspection procedures list bonding and grounding and final inspections, with the equipotential bonding grid checked against NEC Section 680.26.
  • For pools without a cover, the same document requires a fence at least 5 feet high with self-closing, self-latching, lockable gates, under a separate fence permit.

Source: Residential Permits - City of Carmel · Swimming Pool Inspection Procedures - City of Carmel Building and Code Services

Westfield

Community Development DepartmentOnline portal: City of Westfield Citizens Portal

  • Westfield lists a Residential Pool (RP) permit for in-ground pools, submitted through its Citizens Portal after registering an account.
  • Required documents include a site or plot plan showing property lines, existing structures, right-of-way and all easements, with scale, north arrow and address or lot number.
  • The pool page lists a State Construction Design Release (CDR) and an Erosion Control Application among the documents to prepare.
  • Construction documents must include a pool plan and a cross-section drawing of the pool, and review does not guarantee full compliance with the Indiana Building Code.

Source: Pool Permits - City of Westfield · Apply for a Permit - City of Westfield

Indiana state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2020 Indiana Residential Code (675 IAC 14-4.4, based on the 2018 IRC), adopted statewide by the Fire Prevention and Building Safety Commission effective December 26, 2019, with plan review and inspection handled locally.
Pool barriers
Indiana Residential Code Section R326 (675 IAC 14-4.4-38), a state amendment replacing the model pool section, requires a barrier at least 4 ft high around the pool and deck with self-closing, latching gates, or an ASTM F1346 power safety cover.
Contractor licensing
No statewide license for residential pool contractors; Indiana licenses plumbers at state level, and builder licensing is set locally by cities and counties.
Public pools
Public pools, spas and water attractions are built to the Indiana Swimming Pool Code, 675 IAC 20, with design release from the Department of Homeland Security, Division of Fire and Building Safety; operation falls under Indiana Department of Health rule 410 IAC 6-2.1.
Engineer licensing
Indiana State Board of Registration for Professional Engineers

INDIANA QUESTIONS

Questions about Indiana pool plans.

What barrier does Indiana require around a residential pool?

Section R326 of the 2020 Indiana Residential Code requires walls or fencing at least 4 ft high around the pool and deck with self-closing, latching, lockable gates, or a key-operated power safety cover meeting ASTM F1346.

Do public pool plans in Indiana need a design release?

Yes. Public pools and spas need a Construction Design Release from the Division of Fire and Building Safety under 675 IAC 20, with plans sealed by an Indiana registered architect or engineer, before local permits are issued.

Are your Indiana pool plans sealed by a Indiana-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Indiana reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Indiana jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Indiana pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Indiana?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
