KANSAS · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Kansas.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Johnson County (unincorporated areas)

Johnson County Building CodesOnline portal: MGO Connect

  • Any pool 24 inches deep or more needs a permit. The county page covers unincorporated Johnson County only; addresses inside a city go to that city. Applications and plan uploads go through the MGO Connect portal.
  • Required uploads include a site plan, the Land Disturbance Certification and the Residential Swimming Pool APSP-15 Compliance Information Sheet.
  • Concrete and gunite pools need plans designed and sealed by a design professional registered in Kansas. Pre-manufactured liner pools need the manufacturer cut sheet to check lateral loads.
  • The barrier must be shown on the site plan with dimensions, fence type and gates. It must pass final inspection before the pool is filled with water.

Source: Swimming Pool Permit - Johnson County Kansas

Lawrence / Douglas County

City of Lawrence Planning & Development Services, Building Safety Division

  • The city has adopted the International Swimming Pool and Spa Code (ISPSC) for in-ground, above-ground and on-ground pools, hot tubs and spas.
  • The Douglas County Health Department must issue a letter of review and approval before the city issues a pool building permit.
  • The application needs a scaled plot plan (pool, equipment, structures, property lines, easements, setbacks, power lines, barrier) plus pool plans or manufacturer specifications and barrier details.
  • Pools must be in the rear yard, at least 5 feet from all property lines, and not in an easement. The minimum barrier height is 4 feet.

Source: Residential Pools, Hot Tubs and Spas (Lawrence Building Safety guideline)

Kansas state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
No statewide building code; cities and counties adopt their own residential codes, and the State Fire Marshal enforces codes only for state-owned buildings.
Pool barriers
No state statute; residential pool barriers are set by local ordinance.
Contractor licensing
No statewide license for residential pool contractors; licensing is local, for example through county or city contractor licensing programs in the Kansas City and Wichita areas.
Engineer licensing
Kansas State Board of Technical Professions

KANSAS QUESTIONS

Questions about Kansas pool plans.

Which code governs pool drawings in Kansas?

Kansas has no statewide building code. Each city or county adopts its own residential code and pool barrier rules, so drawings are prepared to the code edition and amendments of the jurisdiction issuing the permit.

Who inspects public pools in Johnson County, Kansas?

The Johnson County Department of Health and Environment licenses public and semi-public pools in cities that adopted the county Environmental Sanitary Code, using the 2025 Johnson County Aquatic Health Code. Other cities regulate pools themselves.

Are your Kansas pool plans sealed by a Kansas-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Kansas reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Kansas jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Kansas pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Kansas?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
