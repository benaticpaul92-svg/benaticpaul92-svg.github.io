Kentucky at a Glance

Kentucky pool permit requirements

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2018 Kentucky Residential Code (based on the 2015 IRC, 815 KAR 7:125), mandatory statewide and administered by the Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction.
Pool barriers
Kentucky Residential Code Section R326, including R326.8 barrier requirements for residential swimming pools.
Contractor licensing
No statewide license for residential pool contractors; the Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction licenses plumbing, electrical and HVAC trades, and general builders are regulated locally.
Public pools
Cabinet for Health and Family Services rule 902 KAR 10:120 (Kentucky public swimming and bathing facilities) requires written plan approval before construction, with plans by a Kentucky registered engineer or architect for government pools and pools over 1,600 sq ft.
Engineer licensing
Kentucky State Board of Licensure for Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors
Questions

Frequently Asked

Where are Kentucky residential pool barrier rules found?

The 2018 Kentucky Residential Code, adopted statewide under 815 KAR 7:125, carries residential pool provisions in Section R326, with barrier requirements in R326.8. Local building departments enforce it.

Does a public pool in Kentucky need an engineer's seal?

Under 902 KAR 10:120, pools built by state or local government, and pools over 1,600 sq ft of water surface, need plans prepared and sealed by a Kentucky registered engineer or architect, with written approval before construction.

Are your Kentucky pool plans sealed by a Kentucky-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Kentucky reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Kentucky jurisdiction.
