MARYLAND · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Maryland.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Howard County

Howard County Department of Inspections, Licenses and Permits

  • A plan review memo effective September 7, 2025 applies to all residential swimming pool applications. It states that all plans must be signed and sealed by an engineer.
  • In-ground pool applications need a plot/site plan and verification of compliance with the county checklist based on Chapter 8 of the International Swimming Pool & Spa Code. A certificate of compliance is required at inspection.
  • Above-ground pools need a plot/site plan and the manufacturer's installation guidelines, and also a certificate of compliance at inspection.
  • The county checklist asks for pool shape, wall cross section, floor slope, diving equipment and diving envelope where applicable. Construction plans should reference the current International Swimming Pool & Spa Code.

Source: Requirements for submittal of all residential swimming pool applications (Howard County memo, 2 Sept 2025) · Checklist for Residential Swimming Pool Submittal (Howard County) · Residential Building Permits (Howard County)

Anne Arundel County

Anne Arundel County Inspections and PermitsOnline portal: Land Use Navigator

  • The county lists a pool among residential accessory structures that may need a building permit.
  • Applications, status checks and inspection requests run through the Land Use Navigator system.
  • Submittals include a building permit application, construction plans to a recognized architectural scale, and a site plan at an engineered scale. The site plan must mark easements, property lines, rights of way and any grinder pump, well or septic system.
  • Other attachments, such as a Critical Area Worksheet and a Standard Grading Plan Application, are listed on the page and may not apply to every project.

Source: Residential Accessory Structure (Anne Arundel County)

Maryland state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2021 IRC with state amendments under the Maryland Building Performance Standards (COMAR 09.12.51, Maryland Department of Labor), effective May 29, 2023; local jurisdictions must enforce it and may amend.
Pool barriers
Maryland Swimming Pool and Spa Standards (Public Safety Article §12-1201 et seq., COMAR 09.12.56) adopt the 2021 ISPSC with state amendments for pools with permit applications received on or after December 31, 2023.
Contractor licensing
Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC) license; Business Regulation Article §8-101 counts construction of a swimming pool on land adjacent to a residence as home improvement.
Public pools
Maryland Department of Health, COMAR 10.17.01 Public Swimming Pools and Spas; MDH reviews construction plans from January 1, 2024, unless the local jurisdiction has elected to review them.
Engineer licensing
Maryland State Board for Professional Engineers

MARYLAND QUESTIONS

Questions about Maryland pool plans.

Which pool code applies to a residential pool permit in Maryland?

Pools with permit applications received on or after December 31, 2023 must meet the Maryland Swimming Pool and Spa Standards, COMAR 09.12.56, which adopt the 2021 ISPSC with state amendments. The county or city enforces it.

Does a Maryland residential pool builder need a state license?

Yes. Building a pool adjacent to a residence is home improvement under Business Regulation Article §8-101, so the contractor needs a Maryland Home Improvement Commission license. Public pools also need Maryland Department of Health plan review.

Are your Maryland pool plans sealed by a Maryland-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Maryland reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Maryland jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Maryland pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Maryland?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
