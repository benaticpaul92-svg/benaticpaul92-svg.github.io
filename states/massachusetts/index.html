MASSACHUSETTS · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Massachusetts.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Boston / Suffolk County

Inspectional Services Department (ISD), Building DivisionOnline portal: Inspectional Services Permit Portal

  • Boston requires a Use of Premises Permit to add a pool to a property. The application starts in the ISD online permitting application.
  • The Use of Premises application needs a certified plot plan showing how the land will be used, brought to the Building Division counter.
  • ISD's Swimming Pool Affidavit has the owner certify the pool conforms to the Boston Zoning Code, the Massachusetts State Building Code, IRC Appendix G and IBC Section 3109.
  • The affidavit states the pool must be at least 4 feet from every lot line and fenced at least 6 feet high with a gate locked from the outside.

Source: How to get a use of premises permit (boston.gov) · Swimming Pool Affidavit (ISD) · Construction and Development (boston.gov)

Framingham / Middlesex County

Building and Wire Department

  • Framingham requires a building permit for any structure holding water deeper than 24 inches, including in-ground pools, and a separate electrical permit.
  • The pool guidelines ask for a completed pool permit application, a certified site plan showing the pool, fences and setbacks, and pool elevations and grades.
  • The guidelines call for a barrier at least 48 inches above finished grade, with outward-opening, self-closing and self-latching gates and no opening that passes a 4 inch sphere.
  • Pools may not be used until the Building Department issues an occupancy permit; the guidelines cite 780 CMR and the 2009 IRC Appendix G.

Source: Application for Permit to Install a Swimming Pool (with Swimming Pool Guidelines) · Swimming Pools (Inspectional Services Division)

Plymouth / Plymouth County

Division of Inspectional Services

  • Plymouth's residential pool packet has applicants file a fence affidavit plus zoning, wiring and building applications together. Zoning is processed first, then wiring, then the building permit.
  • The zoning application needs two copies of a site plan showing the pool and its distances from property lines.
  • The packet states the pool must be at least 10 feet from side and rear lines and 20 feet from front lines.
  • The wiring application must be filled out by a licensed electrician, and the building application must describe the pool type, size and fence.

Source: Procedures to Apply for Permits to Erect a Residential Swimming Pool · Permit & License Applications (Plymouth)

Worcester / Worcester County

Inspectional Services Department, Building & Zoning Division

  • Worcester's Building and Zoning Division issues permits for new construction, repairs, additions, alterations and demolition.
  • The Inspectional Services fee schedule lists separate permit lines for above-ground and in-ground pools, so a pool is a permit category there.
  • Worcester's property owner page lists pools among the projects homeowners may need permits for.

Source: Inspectional Services (Worcester) · Information for Property Owners (Worcester) · Department of Inspectional Services Schedule of Fees

Massachusetts state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
780 CMR 10th edition residential volume based on the 2021 IRC with Massachusetts amendments, adopted by the Board of Building Regulations and Standards (BBRS), mandatory statewide.
Pool barriers
780 CMR 51.00 Section R101.4.15 adopts the 2021 ISPSC as amended, so residential pool barriers follow ISPSC Section 305 as amended by Massachusetts.
Contractor licensing
No dedicated state pool contractor license; contractors working on existing owner-occupied 1 to 4 unit homes register as Home Improvement Contractors with the Office of Consumer Affairs and Business Regulation under M.G.L. c. 142A.
Public pools
Massachusetts Department of Public Health, 105 CMR 435.000 Minimum Standards for Swimming Pools (State Sanitary Code Chapter V); plans must be approved in writing by the local Board of Health before construction (105 CMR 435.02).
Engineer licensing
Board of Registration of Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors

MASSACHUSETTS QUESTIONS

Questions about Massachusetts pool plans.

Do Massachusetts in-ground pool drawings need a registered design professional?

Yes. 780 CMR 51.00 Section R107.1 requires construction documents, computations and specifications for in-ground pools to be prepared by a registered design professional. The building official's submission waiver does not apply to in-ground pools.

Which barrier code applies to a Massachusetts residential pool?

The 10th edition of 780 CMR, based on the 2021 IRC, adopts the 2021 ISPSC as amended through Section R101.4.15. Barrier requirements come from ISPSC Section 305 and are enforced by the local building official.

Are your Massachusetts pool plans sealed by a Massachusetts-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Massachusetts reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Massachusetts jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Massachusetts pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Massachusetts?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
