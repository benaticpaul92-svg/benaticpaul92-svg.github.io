NEBRASKA · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Nebraska.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Lincoln / Lancaster County

Lincoln Planning and Development Services, Building and SafetyOnline portal: Lincoln/Lancaster County Citizen Access Portal (Accela)

  • Building permit applications are made through the Citizen Access portal.

Source: Lincoln/Lancaster County Citizen Access Portal · Residential Building - City of Lincoln · Permit Applications - City of Lincoln

Sarpy County (unincorporated areas)

Sarpy County Planning & BuildingOnline portal: Sarpy County Online Permitting System (SmartGov)

  • Sarpy County handles zoning and building outside municipal limits and extraterritorial jurisdiction.
  • Projects inside Papillion, Gretna, Bellevue, Springfield or La Vista go to that city instead.
  • Building permits are applied for online in the county's permitting system, which is powered by SmartGov.
  • The county lists a separate operating permit for public swimming pools and spas; the permit page reviewed has no residential pool detail.

Source: Building Permit - Sarpy County · Online Permitting - Sarpy County · Site map - Sarpy County

Nebraska state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2018 IRC is the state building code (Neb. Rev. Stat. 71-6403); cities, counties and villages that enforce a code must adopt it or a local code that conforms generally (71-6406).
Pool barriers
Set by local ordinance or the locally adopted code; no state pool barrier statute.
Contractor licensing
No state license for residential pool contractors; contractors must register with the Nebraska Department of Labor under the Contractor Registration Act (Neb. Rev. Stat. 48-2104), with local licensing in some cities.
Public pools
Nebraska DHHS, 178 NAC 2 Design, Construction, Operation, and Maintenance of Public Swimming Pools; plans need written DHHS approval and a construction permit before construction (178 NAC 2-003).
Engineer licensing
Nebraska Board of Engineers and Architects

NEBRASKA QUESTIONS

Questions about Nebraska pool plans.

Which residential code do Nebraska jurisdictions use for pool permits?

The state building code is the 2018 IRC under Neb. Rev. Stat. 71-6403. Cities, counties and villages that enforce a code must adopt it or a generally conforming local code, so pool and barrier amendments vary locally.

Who approves public pool plans in Nebraska?

The Department of Health and Human Services. Under 178 NAC 2-003, plans prepared by a design professional are submitted for written approval, and construction cannot begin until DHHS approves final plans and issues a construction permit.

Are your Nebraska pool plans sealed by a Nebraska-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Nebraska reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Nebraska jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Nebraska pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Nebraska?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
