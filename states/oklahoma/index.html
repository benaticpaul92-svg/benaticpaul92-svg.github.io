OKLAHOMA · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Oklahoma.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Oklahoma City

City of Oklahoma City Development Services

  • The city's pool submittal checklist says to submit permits and PDF files online at www.okc.gov/access or in person at 420 W. Main Street, 1st floor.
  • For an outdoor residential pool, the checklist asks for a site plan showing the pool, property lines, distances to lot lines and structures, and all easements and rights-of-way.
  • The checklist also asks for the pool's estimated cost, maximum depth and square footage, and whether it is heated.
  • The residential checklist asks the plans to show the fence enclosure's location, type and height, with a 4 ft minimum height and self-closing, self-latching gates.

Source: Swimming Pool Submittal Requirements (City of Oklahoma City)

Tulsa

City of Tulsa Development Services (Permit Center)Online portal: Citizen Self-Service Portal (CSS)

  • Online applicants choose either Pool (Residential): Pool: In Ground or Pool: Above Ground in the Citizen Self-Service Portal.
  • The residential guidebook asks for a pool wall cross section, the type of construction, drain type and number of drains, pool size and depth, a grading and drainage plan, and an erosion control plan.
  • If the property is on a septic system, a perc test is required, and approval from the City/County Health Department, Environmental Protection, is also required.
  • The guidebook exempts prefabricated pools less than two feet deep from permit requirements, and its site plan affidavit is meant to keep new structures such as pools off easements and rights-of-way.

Source: Residential Building Guidelines / Site Plan Guidebook, January 2025 (City of Tulsa) · Citizen Self Service Portal FAQ (City of Tulsa)

Edmond

City of Edmond Department of Building & Safety

  • Edmond's miscellaneous permits page lists a dimensioned site plan showing distances from property lines to the pool and pool equipment.
  • Edmond's barrier handout cites the 2015 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code and applies to water over 24 inches deep.
  • The handout requires a barrier at least 48 inches above grade, openings that do not pass a 4 inch sphere, and self-closing, self-latching pedestrian gates that open away from the pool.
  • The homeowner acknowledgment says a residential pool cannot be occupied until final inspections are done and a Certificate of Occupancy is issued.

Source: Miscellaneous Permits (City of Edmond) · Swimming Pool Barrier (City of Edmond)

Stillwater

City of Stillwater Development Services Department

  • A building permit is required for pools, spas or hot tubs 24 inches or more deep, or any pool with a filter system, heater or electrical lighting.
  • The pool permit includes the required fence enclosure, and separate fence permits are not issued for it.
  • The plot plan must show distances to property lines, easements, the house, septic lines where there is no public sewer, overhead or underground electric lines, and the enclosure.
  • Pools and decks may not be placed over a platted easement, and pool setbacks follow the accessory structure rules of the zoning district.
  • Decks that support spas or hot tubs must be designed by an engineer, and a bonding inspection is required before concrete is poured.

Source: Informational Guide: Residential Swimming Pools, Hot Tubs and Spas (City of Stillwater) · Residential FAQs for Development Services (City of Stillwater)

Oklahoma state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2024 IRC with Oklahoma amendments (OAC 748:20-6), adopted by the Oklahoma Uniform Building Code Commission as the statewide minimum code effective September 14, 2026.
Pool barriers
The OUBCC-amended IRC pool section requires residential pools, spas and hot tubs needing a permit to be enclosed by a barrier at least 4 ft high with openings that reject a 4 in sphere; lots over 2 acres are exempt (OAC 748:20-6).
Contractor licensing
No statewide license for general or residential pool contractors; the Construction Industries Board licenses the electrical, plumbing and mechanical trades, and pool permits are issued locally.
Public pools
Oklahoma State Department of Health approves new public bathing places before construction under OAC 310:315 (Public Bathing Place Facility Standards), with plans and an engineer's report.
Engineer licensing
Oklahoma State Board of Licensure for Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors

OKLAHOMA QUESTIONS

Questions about Oklahoma pool plans.

Does Oklahoma have a statewide residential pool barrier rule?

Yes. The OUBCC-amended 2024 IRC (OAC 748:20-6, effective September 14, 2026) requires permitted residential pools, spas and hot tubs to be enclosed by a barrier at least 4 ft high with openings that reject a 4 in sphere. Lots over 2 acres are exempt.

Do commercial pools need state approval before construction?

Yes. The Oklahoma State Department of Health must approve each new public bathing place before construction under OAC 310:315. The submittal includes plan sets, the fee and an engineer's report, in addition to the local building permit.

Are your Oklahoma pool plans sealed by a Oklahoma-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Oklahoma reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Oklahoma jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Oklahoma pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Oklahoma?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
