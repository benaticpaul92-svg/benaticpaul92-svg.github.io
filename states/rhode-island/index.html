RHODE ISLAND · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Rhode Island.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Providence

City of Providence Department of Inspection and Standards

  • Providence publishes a Rhode Island Pool Barrier Checklist and says a permit is required for a pool or spa that can hold 24 inches or more of water.
  • The checklist says it incorporates the 2018 edition of the International Swimming Pool and Spa Code.
  • It lists a barrier at least 48 inches tall, no openings that pass a 4 inch sphere, a 36 inch clear zone around pumps and filters, and self-closing, self-latching gates that open away from the pool.
  • Under the zoning section it cites, no private pool or hot tub, including aprons and walks, may be in a front yard or a required corner or interior side setback.
  • The pool and its impervious apron count toward the rear yard and total impervious maximums.

Source: Rhode Island Pool Barrier Checklist (City of Providence), 10.21.25 · Brochures, Applications and FAQ (City of Providence Inspection & Standards)

Cranston

Cranston Department of Building Inspections & Zoning

  • The city's Building Inspection page says its department enforces the current edition of the Rhode Island State Building Code, the International Codes and the National Electrical Code as amended.
  • It says any contractor working on another person's home must be licensed or registered by the State of Rhode Island.
  • The department moved to 155 Gansett Avenue (rear) on March 23, 2026.

Source: Building Inspection (City of Cranston)

Statewide

  • The Rhode Island Swimming Pool and Spa Code (510-RICR-00-00-14) adopts the 2021 edition of the International Swimming Pool and Spa Code, with state amendments. Source: Rhode Island Swimming Pool and Spa Code, 510-RICR-00-00-14 (RI Department of State)

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2021 IRC as the Rhode Island One and Two Family Dwelling Code (RISBC-2, 510-RICR-00-00-2), adopted statewide by the Building Code Standards Committee.
Pool barriers
Statewide Rhode Island Swimming Pool and Spa Code (510-RICR-00-00-14) adopts the 2021 ISPSC, so residential pool barriers follow ISPSC Section 305.
Contractor licensing
Contractors' Registration and Licensing Board registration required before performing, offering or bidding construction work (R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-65-3).
Public pools
Rhode Island Department of Health licenses aquatic venues and reviews construction plans under 216-RICR-50-05-4 (Licensing Aquatic Venues).
Engineer licensing
Rhode Island Board of Registration for Professional Engineers

RHODE ISLAND QUESTIONS

Questions about Rhode Island pool plans.

Which code governs residential pool barriers in Rhode Island?

The Rhode Island Swimming Pool and Spa Code, 510-RICR-00-00-14, adopts the 2021 ISPSC statewide, so barriers follow ISPSC Section 305. Providence also publishes a pool barrier checklist built on Section 305 and its zoning rules.

Does a pool contractor need state registration in Rhode Island?

Yes. R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-65-3 requires a current certificate of registration from the Contractors' Registration and Licensing Board before a contractor performs, offers or bids construction work on a structure.

Are your Rhode Island pool plans sealed by a Rhode Island-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Rhode Island reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Rhode Island jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Rhode Island pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Rhode Island?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
