Rhode Island pool permit requirements

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2021 IRC as the Rhode Island One and Two Family Dwelling Code (RISBC-2, 510-RICR-00-00-2), adopted statewide by the Building Code Standards Committee.
Pool barriers
Statewide Rhode Island Swimming Pool and Spa Code (510-RICR-00-00-14) adopts the 2021 ISPSC, so residential pool barriers follow ISPSC Section 305.
Contractor licensing
Contractors' Registration and Licensing Board registration required before performing, offering or bidding construction work (R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-65-3).
Public pools
Rhode Island Department of Health licenses aquatic venues and reviews construction plans under 216-RICR-50-05-4 (Licensing Aquatic Venues).

Questions

Frequently Asked

Which code governs residential pool barriers in Rhode Island?

The Rhode Island Swimming Pool and Spa Code, 510-RICR-00-00-14, adopts the 2021 ISPSC statewide, so barriers follow ISPSC Section 305. Providence also publishes a pool barrier checklist built on Section 305 and its zoning rules.

Does a pool contractor need state registration in Rhode Island?

Yes. R.I. Gen. Laws § 5-65-3 requires a current certificate of registration from the Contractors' Registration and Licensing Board before a contractor performs, offers or bids construction work on a structure.

Are your Rhode Island pool plans sealed by a Rhode Island-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Rhode Island reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Rhode Island jurisdiction.
