SOUTH CAROLINA · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for South Carolina.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Charleston

City of Charleston Permit Center (Development Services)Online portal: Customer Self Service Portal (CSS)

  • Residential and commercial swimming pool permits can be submitted online through the Customer Self Service Portal (CSS).
  • The May 2026 residential pool guidance requires a separate electrical sub permit, and a gas sub permit for a pool heater, and says the city prohibits additional electrical meters for a pool or spa.
  • The site plan must show pool dimensions, equipment location, distances to lot lines, barrier locations and grand trees of 24 inch D.B.H. or larger; Daniel Island plans also need ARB approval.
  • Pool and spa structural plans must be signed and sealed by a licensed South Carolina structural engineer and address buoyancy from the high seasonal water table.
  • The city's express in-person pool review applies only to in-ground pools outside a flood zone and outside BAR or DRB areas.

Source: General Permitting Guidance: Residential Swimming Pools and Spas, May 2026 (City of Charleston) · Permit Center (City of Charleston) · Applications & Guidelines (City of Charleston)

Charleston County (unincorporated)

Charleston County Building Inspection Services

  • The county publishes a V-Zone Design Certificate for Pools and Spas in V-Zones and Coastal A Zones, with pre-construction and as-built versions.
  • The certificate must be signed and sealed by a registered professional engineer or architect, and construction plans must match it.
  • It cites ASCE 24-14 Section 9.6.2 and requires the pool to be structurally independent of nearby buildings and not subject to flotation that damages foundations.
  • It asks for base and design flood elevations in NAVD 88 and scour and embedment depths for the pool and equipment stand.

Source: Charleston County V-Zone Design Certificate for Pools and Spas · Forms (Charleston County Building Inspection Services)

Mount Pleasant

Town of Mount Pleasant Building Inspections (Planning, Land Use, and Neighborhoods)Online portal: OPAL

  • The town takes planning, building, code enforcement and business license applications through its online OPAL portal, and applicants must register first.
  • Tree removals tied to residential construction, including pools, are reviewed as part of the building permit application rather than a separate tree permit.

Source: Apply for Permits and Boards and Commissions (Town of Mount Pleasant)

South Carolina state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2021 IRC with South Carolina modifications, mandatory for all counties and municipalities since January 1, 2023 (South Carolina Building Codes Council).
Pool barriers
No state pool barrier statute; the ISPSC is a permissive code in South Carolina, so barrier rules apply where a county or city adopts the ISPSC or its own ordinance.
Contractor licensing
Since May 19, 2023, residential pool work over $10,000 requires a General Contractor license with the Swimming Pools classification from the Contractor's Licensing Board, or a Residential Builder license from the Residential Builders Commission (LLR).
Public pools
South Carolina Department of Environmental Services, Regulation 61-51 (Public Swimming Pools): a permit to construct is required, with plans sealed by a South Carolina registered architect or engineer.
Engineer licensing
South Carolina Board of Registration for Professional Engineers and Surveyors

SOUTH CAROLINA QUESTIONS

Questions about South Carolina pool plans.

What license does a residential pool builder need in South Carolina?

Since May 19, 2023, residential pool construction above $10,000 requires a General Contractor license with the Swimming Pools classification from the Contractor's Licensing Board, or a Residential Builder license from the Residential Builders Commission.

Do public pool plans need a South Carolina engineer's seal?

Yes. Regulation 61-51 requires a permit to construct before a public pool is built or altered, and plans must be prepared, stamped, dated and signed by an architect or engineer registered in South Carolina.

Are your South Carolina pool plans sealed by a South Carolina-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in South Carolina reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your South Carolina jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare South Carolina pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in South Carolina?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
