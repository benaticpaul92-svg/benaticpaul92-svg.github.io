SOUTH DAKOTA · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for South Dakota.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Sioux Falls

Planning & Development Services (Building Services)Online portal: Customer Self Service (CSS)

  • Residential permit applications are filed online through the Customer Self Service portal, or on paper at the City Center counter with an 11x17 plan set.
  • Residential permits are issued only to licensed residential contractors, owners who live in the structure, or owners of registered residential rentals.
  • Plan review can involve Building, Planning, Zoning, Flood Plain, Historic Preservation and Engineering staff, depending on the project.
  • The city lists the 2024 IBC, IRC and ISPSC as adopted, with local amendments, plus the 2023 NEC and the 2024 Uniform Plumbing Code.
  • The city's residential pool guide requires a barrier at least 48 inches high, with no gaps over 4 inches, installed, inspected and approved before the pool is filled.
  • Barrier gates must be self-closing and self-latching, and the guide treats swim spas as swimming pools for permitting and barrier rules.

Source: Building Permits - City of Sioux Falls · HOW TO: Apply for Residential Plan Review & Permit Issuance · Adopted Building Codes - City of Sioux Falls · Residential Swimming Pools, Hot Tubs, and Spas Guide

Minnehaha County (unincorporated areas around Sioux Falls)

Minnehaha County Planning & Zoning

  • Planning & Zoning handles zoning and building permits and inspects new construction in the unincorporated part of the county.
  • The county adopted the 2021 International Residential Code by Ordinance MC 62-22, with local amendments.
  • The county's amended code counts any structure holding water more than 18 inches deep as a swimming pool, including in-ground pools and hot tubs.
  • Its pool barrier must be at least 42 inches above grade and be installed, inspected and approved before the pool is filled with water.

Source: Planning & Zoning - Minnehaha County · Ordinance MC 62-22, 2021 International Residential Code

Aberdeen

City of Aberdeen Building Inspection Department

  • The Building Inspection Department is on the third floor of City Hall at 123 South Lincoln Street.
  • The city requires a permit to construct, enlarge, alter or move a building, structure or property, and all fencing needs a permit.
  • Prefabricated swimming pools holding no more than 5,000 gallons are listed as not needing a city permit.
  • Electrical permit questions go to the South Dakota Electrical Commission, not the city, according to the city's permit handout.

Source: Permit Requirements (City of Aberdeen)

Pennington County (Rapid City area, unincorporated)

Pennington County Planning & Zoning

  • The county's development process ends with a Building Permit and Certificate of Compliance.
  • The county requires a pre-development meeting with county staff to learn which steps and submittals apply to a project.
  • The county posts a Building Permit Application (Residential) as a downloadable form.

Source: Development Guide - Pennington County · Permits & Forms - Pennington County

South Dakota state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
No mandatory statewide residential code; under SDCL 11-10-12 a municipality may adopt the 2024 IRC but may not impose stricter residential requirements.
Pool barriers
Set by local ordinance; there is no state residential pool barrier statute or statewide pool code.
Contractor licensing
No statewide license for general or residential pool contractors; plumbing and electrical work are licensed at state level, and pool permits are issued locally.
Engineer licensing
South Dakota Board of Technical Professions

SOUTH DAKOTA QUESTIONS

Questions about South Dakota pool plans.

Is there a statewide residential building code in South Dakota?

No. Under SDCL 11-10-12 a municipality may adopt the 2024 IRC, but it may not adopt residential requirements more stringent than that code. Pool permits, barriers and inspections depend on the city or county ordinance.

Who regulates pools at hotels and campgrounds in South Dakota?

The South Dakota Department of Health licenses lodging and campground establishments and reviews their pools and spas through a water recreational facility plan review questionnaire. Pool provisions sit in the ARSD Article 44:02 establishment rules.

Are your South Dakota pool plans sealed by a South Dakota-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in South Dakota reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your South Dakota jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare South Dakota pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in South Dakota?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
