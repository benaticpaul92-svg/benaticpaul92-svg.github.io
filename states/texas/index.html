TEXAS · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Texas.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Houston / Harris County

Houston Health Department and Houston Permitting CenterOnline portal: Houston Permit Portal

  • The Houston Health Department says it reviews and regulates all new residential swimming pool and spa construction in the city.
  • New residential pools must meet the Texas State standards for public pools and spas, according to the Houston Health Department.
  • Applicants submit the city's Minimum Standards for Residential Pool/Spa Plan Submission document with their plans, taken to the Houston Permitting Center at 1002 Washington Avenue.
  • To close out a project, the owner emails pools@houstontx.gov with the project number and address, after the Structural group has approved the project.

Source: Swimming Pool (Residential) Standards - Houston Health Department

Dallas / Dallas County

Dallas Planning and Development DepartmentOnline portal: DallasNow

  • The Dallas Planning and Development guide says in-ground pools and spas always need a Residential Pool/Spa Permit.
  • A pool barrier fence needs its own Residential Accessory Structure Permit, and all pool electrical work needs a separate Electrical Permit.
  • Applications are submitted through DallasNow, the city's online permitting system.
  • Dallas Chapter 62 adopts the 2021 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code, effective June 13, 2022.

Source: Dallas Planning & Development Guide

Austin / Travis County

City of Austin Development Services Department, Residential Plan ReviewOnline portal: Austin Build + Connect (AB+C) Portal

  • Residential pools are applied for through the Pool/Uncovered Deck (Pool is Small Project) Web Form on the Residential Plan Review page.
  • Application status is checked, and approved permits are activated, through the Austin Build + Connect (AB+C) Portal.
  • Extra reviews can apply: floodplain review if the lot is in or within 100 feet of the 100-year floodplain, and on-site sewage review if the lot has a septic system.
  • Austin exempts a prefabricated residential pool less than 24 inches deep from a building permit, but a pool in a floodplain may still need one.

Source: Residential Plan Review - City of Austin · Work Exempt from Building Permits - City of Austin

San Antonio / Bexar County

City of San Antonio Development Services DepartmentOnline portal: BuildSA Customer Portal

  • Residential pools are filed in the BuildSA Customer Portal as a Residential Improvements Permit Application with the Swimming Pool or Spa subtype.
  • The submittal is a to-scale site plan showing the lot, existing structures, easements, the pool and the barrier, plus the signed Swimming Pool/Spa Barriers and Safety Devices Compliance Agreement.
  • A plat is not required, but the pool waterline must be at least 5 feet from the property line.
  • San Antonio requires compliance with the 2024 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code and the 2024 IECC as amended by the city; outdoor residential pools need a barrier at least 48 inches high.

Source: Information Bulletin 170, Swimming Pool and Spa Permits (May 2025)

Fort Worth / Tarrant County

City of Fort Worth Development ServicesOnline portal: Accela (City of Fort Worth permit portal)

  • Fort Worth lists swimming pools among the accessory structures that need a residential building permit, and permits may be applied for online.
  • The pool packet asks for a recorded plat, a survey or site plan showing setbacks, lot lines and easements with a north arrow and scale, and construction plans.
  • Pools must meet the 2018 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code, with sections of the 2021 International Plumbing Code, the 2023 National Electrical Code and Fort Worth amendments.
  • Private residential pools must submit an entrapment form, and plans must be stamped and signed off by Oncor Electric before submittal.

Source: Residential Permitting - Fort Worth Development Services · Residential Pools Checklist and Packet (Pools Only)

Frisco / Collin and Denton Counties

City of Frisco Health & Food SafetyOnline portal: City of Frisco Online Application System

  • Frisco requires a pool construction permit for in-ground and above-ground pools, spas holding more than 24 inches of water, and splash pads, applied for online.
  • The drawing package includes a plot plan with setbacks and equipment location, a pool profile, and a utility provider stamp; all files are uploaded as PDFs.
  • A Professional Engineer letter is required if the pool beam encroaches the 1:1 ratio to the nearest foundation, and engineer drawings are required for piers, retaining walls and structures over 36 inches high.
  • Frisco's checklist prohibits pool construction in a floodway or 100-year floodplain and requires the water's edge to be at least 3 feet from any property line.
  • The barrier must be 48 to 96 inches high, chain link is prohibited, and the enclosure follows the 2018 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code with amendments.

Source: Pools & Pool Enclosures - City of Frisco · Residential Pools, Spas, Fountains and Splashpads Submittal Requirements & Inspection Checklist (July 2026) · Swimming Pool & Spa Enclosure / Specific Code Requirements

Sugar Land / Fort Bend County

City of Sugar Land Permits and Inspections

  • Sugar Land's residential pool page lists a survey showing the pool location and its distances to property lines, setbacks and easements.
  • A licensed Professional Engineer's sealed plans are required if the pool or spa is 5 feet or closer to a structure.
  • Other listed items include an HOA approval letter, drain and VGB compliance information, overflow and drainage details, and an electrical load analysis from a Master Electrician.
  • The page asks for plans to note the 2024 International Residential Code, the 2024 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code and the 2023 National Electrical Code.
  • Pools cannot be located within recorded easements or within 3 feet of a lot line, and plumbing and electrical contractors pull separate permits after the pool permit is issued.

Source: Residential Permit Categories - City of Sugar Land · General Residential Swimming Pool Guidelines - City of Sugar Land

Round Rock / Williamson County

City of Round Rock Building Inspection DivisionOnline portal: Round Rock Permit Portal

  • Round Rock takes pool permit applications through its online Permit Portal at permits.roundrocktexas.gov, where inspections are also scheduled.
  • Required documents are a full set of construction plans, a property survey showing the pool location and its distance to each property line, and electrical or plumbing details where applicable.
  • Pool plans must comply with the 2024 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code, which the city calls the Pool Code.
  • The general contractor and any electrical or plumbing subcontractors must confirm their assignments in the portal before the permit is issued.

Source: Homeowner Projects - City of Round Rock · Other Building Permits - City of Round Rock

TEXAS QUESTIONS

Questions about Texas pool plans.

Which Texas cities do you cover?

All of them. Houston, Dallas, Austin and San Antonio come up most often and we hold their plan-review checklists, but we build to whichever jurisdiction the project sits in.

Do you produce SWPPP documentation?

Yes, where the disturbed area triggers TPDES requirements. It is one of the more common reasons Texas pool submittals get held up.

How fast can you prepare Texas pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Texas?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
