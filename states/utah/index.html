UTAH · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Utah.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Utah County (unincorporated areas)

Utah County Community Development, Building Division

  • The Building Division handles permit applications, plan reviews and inspections only in the unincorporated parts of Utah County; properties inside a city use that city's office.
  • Every building permit needs the county's Building Permit Application (All Projects), and applications can be submitted in person or electronically.
  • The county lists the 2021 International Residential Code, the 2024 International Building Code and the 2023 National Electrical Code under Utah Code Title 15A.
  • The county's page lists frost depth as 30 in. or an engineer's design if deeper.

Source: Utah County Community Development · Utah County Community Development, Building

West Jordan (Salt Lake County)

West Jordan Community Development, Building & SafetyOnline portal: West Jordan Development Public Portal

  • Building permit applications are submitted online through the West Jordan Public Portal, which requires a registered account.
  • The Building & Safety page offers online submittal instructions, a site plan information handout, a Codes & Design Criteria sheet and an owner/builder certification form.
  • A property owner affidavit must be downloaded and submitted with the permit application.

Source: West Jordan Building & Safety · West Jordan Development Application and Permits

Utah state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2021 IRC with Utah amendments, mandatory statewide as part of the Utah State Construction Code adopted by the Legislature (Utah Code 15A-2-103).
Pool barriers
Statewide: the Utah Construction Code adopts the residential provisions of the 2024 ISPSC (Utah Code 15A-2-103, amendments in Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 10), so barriers follow ISPSC Section 305.
Contractor licensing
Utah Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL): pool builders hold S330 Landscape and Recreation Contractor (covers swimming pools and spas) or B100 General Building, with plumbing and electrical by licensed trades.
Public pools
Utah Admin. Code R392-302 (Design, Construction and Operation of Public Pools): plans sealed by a Utah-licensed engineer or architect, reviewed by the local health department within 30 days.
Engineer licensing
Professional Engineers and Professional Land Surveyors Licensing Board (Utah DOPL)

UTAH QUESTIONS

Questions about Utah pool plans.

Which pool code applies to residential pools in Utah?

The Utah State Construction Code adopts the residential provisions of the 2024 ISPSC statewide, with Utah amendments in Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 10, alongside the 2021 IRC. Salt Lake City requires barrier details on every pool permit plan.

Do public pool plans in Utah need a Utah-licensed engineer?

Yes. Rule R392-302 requires public pool plans to carry the seal of a designing professional licensed by Utah DOPL. The local health department reviews the plans and issues findings within 30 days of submittal.

Are your Utah pool plans sealed by a Utah-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Utah reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Utah jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Utah pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Utah?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
