UTAH · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Pool permit notes for Salt Lake City, West Jordan, Herriman and more in Utah.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on November 15, 2026 unless a card gives a later date. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your request.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the quote form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Jump to: Salt Lake City West Jordan Herriman Utah County Eagle Mountain Lehi

Salt Lake City pool permit notes

Salt Lake City Building Services & Civil Enforcement
  • Residential pools and spas deeper than 24 inches are regulated by the 2024 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code (ISPSC) and need a Salt Lake City permit.
  • The pool permit plans must show the barrier requirements, even if the pool contractor is not building the barrier.
2 more permit notes
  • A pool permit is not closed until all barrier requirements are met.
  • The pool checklist is uploaded as a PDF to the City Required Forms folder in ProjectDox. The site plan must show how backwash and drain water is discharged, under ISPSC Section 321 as amended by Utah.

Source: Salt Lake City Building Services, Private Residential Pool/Spa Login Checklist · Salt Lake City Building Services, Applicable Building and Fire Codes · Checked November 18, 2026

West Jordan (Salt Lake County) pool permit notes

West Jordan Community Development, Building & Safety

West Jordan Development Public Portal

  • Building permit applications are submitted online through the West Jordan Public Portal, which requires a registered account.
  • The Building & Safety page offers online submittal instructions, a site plan information handout, a Codes & Design Criteria sheet and an owner/builder certification form.
1 more permit note
  • A property owner affidavit must be downloaded and submitted with the permit application.

Source: West Jordan Building & Safety · West Jordan Development Application and Permits

Herriman pool permit notes

Herriman City Building Department
  • A permit is required for a pool or spa 24 inches deep or more, requested through the city’s online Permit Application Form.
  • The application needs a pool site plan and complete construction plans that follow the city’s Residential Swimming Pool Checklist.
2 more permit notes
  • A gas pool heater needs a gas line diagram. An electric pool heater needs a one-line diagram or total electrical load calculations.
  • A pool in a public utility easement needs easement waivers from all utilities, with copies submitted with the application, and the pool needs a final inspection before use.

Source: Herriman City, Swimming Pools and Spas · Checked November 18, 2026

Utah County (unincorporated areas) pool permit notes

Utah County Community Development, Building Division
  • The Building Division handles permit applications, plan reviews and inspections only in the unincorporated parts of Utah County; properties inside a city use that city’s office.
  • Every building permit needs the county’s Building Permit Application (All Projects), and applications can be submitted in person or electronically.
2 more permit notes
  • The county lists the 2021 International Residential Code, the 2024 International Building Code and the 2023 National Electrical Code under Utah Code Title 15A.
  • The county’s page lists frost depth as 30 in. or an engineer’s design if deeper.

Source: Utah County Community Development · Utah County Community Development, Building

Eagle Mountain pool permit notes

Eagle Mountain City Building Department
  • A building permit is required for pools 24 inches or deeper, or pools with a filter system, heater or electrical lighting.
  • The city lists these pool submittals: permit application, site plan with dimensions and pool location, detailed plans, required engineering documents, and electrical and gas diagrams.
2 more permit notes
  • For residential pools over 24 inches deep, the city’s barrier checklist calls for a barrier at least 48 inches high with self-closing, self-latching gates that open outward.
  • The city’s barrier checklist refers to Section 3109 of the 2021 International Building Code for detailed requirements.

Source: Eagle Mountain City Building Department · Eagle Mountain Swimming Pool Enclosure Requirements (PDF) · Checked November 18, 2026

Lehi (Utah County) pool permit notes

Lehi City Building and Inspections Department

Building Permit Submittal Portal

  • Under Section 26.100 of the Lehi City Development Code, swimming pools must be set back at least four feet from side and rear property lines and must not be located within any required utility easements.
  • Section 12.020(E)(2) of the code lets underground structures such as swimming pools sit in a required rear or side yard if they are no more than two feet above adjacent grade and no closer than four feet to any property line.
2 more permit notes
  • The city says its Building and Inspections Department handles and issues all building permits for Lehi City, and all permit applications must be submitted through the Building Permit Submittal Portal.
  • The city says all plans, including permit applications, compliance checks, and structural and geotech reports, are submitted electronically and reviewed digitally, and contractors are responsible for printing a hard copy of the approved plans showing the city’s stamp and having it on the job site for every inspection.

Source: Chapter 26 Accessory Uses, Buildings and Structures (Lehi City Development Code) · Chapter 12 Property Development Standards (Lehi City Development Code) · Building and Inspections (Lehi City) · Checked November 25, 2026

Utah state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so name your permit office in your request.

Residential code
2021 IRC with Utah amendments, mandatory statewide as part of the Utah State Construction Code adopted by the Legislature (Utah Code 15A-2-103).
Pool barriers
Statewide: the Utah Construction Code adopts the residential provisions of the 2024 ISPSC (Utah Code 15A-2-103, amendments in Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 10), so barriers follow ISPSC Section 305.
Contractor licensing
Utah Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL): pool builders hold S330 Landscape and Recreation Contractor (covers swimming pools and spas) or B100 General Building, with plumbing and electrical by licensed trades.
Public pools
Utah Admin. Code R392-302 (Design, Construction and Operation of Public Pools): plans sealed by a Utah-licensed engineer or architect, reviewed by the local health department within 30 days.
Engineer licensing
Professional Engineers and Professional Land Surveyors Licensing Board (Utah DOPL)

UTAH QUESTIONS

Questions about Utah pool plans.

Which pool code applies to residential pools in Utah?

The Utah State Construction Code adopts the residential provisions of the 2024 ISPSC statewide, with Utah amendments in Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 10, alongside the 2021 IRC. Salt Lake City requires barrier details on every pool permit plan.

Do public pool plans in Utah need a Utah-licensed engineer?

Yes. Rule R392-302 requires public pool plans to carry the seal of a designing professional licensed by Utah DOPL. The local health department reviews the plans and issues findings within 30 days of submittal.

Can my Utah pool plans be sealed by an engineer licensed in Utah?

Only when a Professional Engineer licensed in Utah takes your project and directs the engineering while the drawings are prepared. JEBN prepares the drawings and does not seal them.

What if plan review comes back with comments?

Send us the comments. Your quote states the revisions included, and we identify the sheets each comment affects. Comments on the engineering go to the engineer of record.

How fast can you prepare Utah pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. Where your permit needs an engineer’s seal, the engineer’s work takes additional time, and so does the permit office’s own review.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Utah?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings that respond to it.
