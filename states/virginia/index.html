VIRGINIA · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Virginia.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Fairfax County

Fairfax County Land Development ServicesOnline portal: Planning and Land Use System (PLUS)

  • Residential pool permits come from Land Development Services; the Health Department does not issue permits for or inspect private residential pools, spas and hot tubs.
  • The county lists pools and spas under its residential miscellaneous building permit.
  • A fence used as a barrier around a swimming pool needs a permit, even though most residential fences do not.
  • Permit applications are made in PLUS, the county's online Planning and Land Use System.

Source: Swimming Pool Codes and Permits (Fairfax County Health Department) · Permit Library: Residential Building · When a Permit is Required

Loudoun County

Loudoun County Building and DevelopmentOnline portal: LandMARC

  • A pool needs both a building permit and a zoning permit, plus a plat showing the pool, its distances to side and rear lines, and the equipment location.
  • The county requires a pool fence under the 2021 International Swimming Pool and Spa Code and a signed Pool Fence Acknowledgement Form.
  • A grading permit is needed if more than 5,000 square feet is disturbed; electrical and gas permits may also be required.
  • In an incorporated town, an approved town zoning permit must be submitted; lots on well and septic need Health Department approval first.
  • Required building inspections are steel, perimeter bonding and final; applications and inspection scheduling are in LandMARC.

Source: Pool Permits (Loudoun County)

Virginia Beach

Permits and Inspections Division, Department of Planning and Community DevelopmentOnline portal: Accela Citizen Access (Permits & Inspections online permitting)

  • A building permit is required for in-ground and above-ground pools and hot tubs or spas able to hold at least 24 inches of water.
  • In-ground pools have setback/footing and final building inspections; above-ground pools have a final inspection.
  • New structures in the Chesapeake Bay Preservation Area, flood zones or Southern Rivers Watershed Area may need extra environmental approvals before the building permit.
  • The city enforces the Virginia Construction, Residential and Property Maintenance Codes for new pools and barriers.

Source: Residential Building Permits (Virginia Beach) · Swimming Pool Safety (Virginia Beach Housing and Neighborhood Preservation)

Chesterfield County

Chesterfield County Building Inspection DepartmentOnline portal: Accela Citizen Access

  • The county's December 2021 pool guide says the state building code requires a permit for pools over 150 square feet, over 5,000 gallons, or 24 inches or more deep.
  • Applications need a plot plan with distances from the pool to all property lines and the house, plus two sets of construction drawings.
  • In-ground pool drawings must show wall sections with reinforcing steel size and spacing and be sealed by a Virginia registered design professional.
  • The pool, barrier, electrical and (if gas-heated) gas work are permitted separately, and the guide tells applicants to check setbacks and easements with the Planning Department.

Source: Requirements for Outdoor Residential Swimming Pools (Chesterfield County, Dec 2021)

Virginia state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
2021 Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code (Virginia Residential Code based on the 2021 IRC), mandatory statewide since January 18, 2024; adopted by the Board of Housing and Community Development.
Pool barriers
Virginia Residential Code Section R327 requires pools and spas to comply with the 2021 ISPSC, with barriers under ISPSC Section 305.
Contractor licensing
Virginia Board for Contractors (DPOR) licenses pool builders under the Swimming Pool Construction (POL) specialty, also within the RBC and CBC classifications (18VAC50-22-30).
Public pools
Virginia Department of Health rule 12VAC5-462 covers only posting of water quality results; public pool construction follows the USBC, and new VDH pool regulations under Va. Code § 32.1-248.1 are in development (2026).
Engineer licensing
Virginia Board for Architects, Professional Engineers, Land Surveyors, Certified Interior Designers and Landscape Architects (APELSCIDLA Board, DPOR)

VIRGINIA QUESTIONS

Questions about Virginia pool plans.

Which code governs residential pool barriers in Virginia?

Section R327 of the 2021 Virginia Residential Code, part of the USBC, requires pools and spas to comply with the 2021 ISPSC. Barrier requirements are in ISPSC Section 305 and are enforced by local building officials statewide.

What license does a Virginia pool builder need?

The Board for Contractors (DPOR) licenses pool builders. The Swimming Pool Construction (POL) specialty covers in-ground pools, and the license class depends on contract size. Trade plumbing, electrical and HVAC work is excluded from POL and needs its own licensing.

Are your Virginia pool plans sealed by a Virginia-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Virginia reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Virginia jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Virginia pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Virginia?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
