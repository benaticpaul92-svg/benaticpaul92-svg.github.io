WASHINGTON · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Pool permit notes for King County, Seattle, Kennewick and more in Washington.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on November 15, 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your request.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the quote form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Jump to: King County Seattle Kennewick Vancouver

King County (unincorporated areas) pool permit notes

King County Department of Local Services, Permitting Division
  • Pools accessory to single-family homes and duplexes are exempt from a building permit only if prefabricated, entirely above ground, under 24 inches deep and 5,000 gallons or less, or used temporarily under three months a year.
  • A pool permit submittal needs a site plan, structural working drawings with engineering calculations or an approved ICC-ES report, and the pool and barrier layout at 1/4 inch = 1 ft.
3 more permit notes
  • Energy code information on pool heaters, controls, covers and pumps must be provided with the submittal.
  • Properties on a septic system need Public Health approval of the pool location before the building permit application.
  • Single-family residence pools for the family's own use are exempt from Public Health plan review and permits.

Source: Private Swimming Pools or Spas (King County Permitting, Aug 2023) · Plan review and operating permits for water recreation facilities (King County)

Seattle pool permit notes

Seattle Department of Construction and Inspections (SDCI)
  • Seattle has adopted the 2021 International Residential Code with local amendments, published as the Seattle Residential Code.
  • The 2021 Seattle Residential Code exempts prefabricated pools with walls entirely above adjacent grade and capacity of 5,000 gallons or less from permits.
2 more permit notes
  • SDCI lists accessory swimming pools among standard-plan types and says those plans must be stamped by a Washington state structural engineer with structural notes, soil assumptions and calculations.
  • The Seattle Residential Code lists Appendix G, Swimming Pools, Spas and Hot Tubs, among its appendices.

Source: Residential Code (Seattle SDCI) · 2021 Seattle Residential Code, Chapter 1 Administration · SDCI Tip 310 Standard Plans

Kennewick (Tri-Cities) pool permit notes

City of Kennewick Building Safety Division

Online portal: Customer Self Service

  • Pool permits include an application, a scaled site plan showing pool, fence and heater, and a mechanical permit application if the pool is heated.
  • The city asks for an ICC-ES report for liner or fiberglass pools, or engineering for a poured-in-place pool.
3 more permit notes
  • The city's express checklist for pools is online-only; it also asks for plot plan details such as slopes, easements, setbacks, barrier and equipment locations.
  • Addresses on septic need an approval letter and stamped plot plan from the Benton Franklin Health Department before applying.
  • The city's pool requirements sheet cites the 2015 ISPSC for barrier rules, so the edition should be confirmed with the city.

Source: Residential Swimming Pool Requirements (Kennewick Building Safety Division) · EXPRESS Residential Permit Submittal Checklist, Swimming Pools and Spas (Kennewick)

Vancouver pool permit notes

City of Vancouver Community Development Department

Online portal: Avolve ePlans

  • Residential building permits (RES) are required for construction, alteration, addition, repair or demolition of residential buildings and structures.
  • Plans are uploaded through the city's plan submittal site, Avolve ePlans, after the application form is sent to the permit specialists.
1 more permit note
  • The city publishes a Swimming Pool Enclosures and Safety Devices Requirements document among its residential permit forms.

Source: Residential Building Permits (City of Vancouver, WA)

Washington state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so name your permit office in your request.

Residential code
2021 IRC with Washington amendments (WAC 51-51), mandatory statewide since March 15, 2024, adopted by the State Building Code Council; 2024 editions take effect May 3, 2027.
Pool barriers
WAC 51-51-0327 (IRC R327) requires single-family and owner-occupied duplex pools to comply with the 2021 ISPSC, including its barrier provisions.
Contractor licensing
No pool-specific license; contractors must register with the Washington State Department of Labor & Industries as general or specialty contractors (RCW 18.27).
Public pools
Washington State Department of Health, WAC 246-260 (Water Recreation Facilities): construction permit from DOH or the local health jurisdiction, with plans stamped by an engineer or architect (WAC 246-260-021).
Engineer licensing
Washington State Board of Registration for Professional Engineers and Land Surveyors

WASHINGTON QUESTIONS

Questions about Washington pool plans.

Which code applies to a residential pool in Washington?

WAC 51-51-0327 amends the 2021 IRC so pools at single-family homes and owner-occupied duplexes must comply with the 2021 ISPSC. The 2024 code editions take effect May 3, 2027. The local building department issues the permit.

Do hotel, apartment or club pools need health department approval in Washington?

Yes. Water recreation facilities under WAC 246-260 need a construction permit from the Department of Health or local health jurisdiction before work starts, with plans prepared, stamped and signed by an engineer or architect.

Can my Washington pool plans be sealed by an engineer licensed in Washington?

Only when a Professional Engineer licensed in Washington takes your project. JEBN prepares the drawings and does not seal them.

What if plan review comes back with comments?

Send us the comments. Your quote states the revision support included, and we identify the sheets and calculations each comment affects.

How fast can you prepare Washington pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Washington?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
