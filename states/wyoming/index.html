WYOMING · CITY AND COUNTY PERMIT NOTES

Local permit notes for Wyoming.

Each city and county runs its own pool permit review. These notes come from the pages each office publishes, checked on 15 November 2026. Rules change, so open the linked source or send us the current checklist with your enquiry.

Your city or county is not listed? Name the permit office in the enquiry form. We draw to the checklist it publishes.

Cheyenne (Laramie County)

City of Cheyenne Building Permitting and Licensing DivisionOnline portal: OpenGov Permits & Licensing

  • The city says its Building Division is fully online: all permit applications and inspection requests go through the OpenGov portal.
  • The city lists the 2024 IBC, 2024 IRC and 2024 IPC among its adopted ICC codes, with the 2018 IECC and 2023 NEC.
  • The city will adopt the 2026 NEC on January 1, 2027, and accepts plans under either the 2023 or 2026 NEC until then.

Source: Building Permitting & Licensing (City of Cheyenne) · ICC 2024 Building Codes (City of Cheyenne) · Plan Review Information (City of Cheyenne, 2026)

Laramie (Albany County)

City of Laramie Building Department (Code Administration)Online portal: Civic Access web portal

  • The city requires building permits for most residential projects and publishes a short list of exceptions; it tells applicants to contact Code Administration if unsure.
  • Building, electrical, mechanical, plumbing and sign permits must all be applied for through the Civic Access web portal.
  • The city publishes informational bulletins on adopted codes and design criteria, plan and drawing standards, and site plan requirements.

Source: Building Permits and Informational Bulletins (City of Laramie)

Jackson (Teton County)

Town of Jackson Planning & Building Department

  • The town's one and two family building permit application is submitted by email to townbuilding@jacksonwy.gov.
  • That application says separate permits are required for plumbing, mechanical and electrical work.
  • Grading information is submitted with the building permit; plan-level grading information must be prepared by a Wyoming-registered professional engineer or landscape architect unless exempted.

Source: One and Two Family Building Permit Application (Town of Jackson, effective June 2026) · Permits and Applications (Town of Jackson)

Wyoming state-level rules

The state-level rules your drawings are checked against. Cities and counties can add local amendments, so send the jurisdiction with your enquiry.

Residential code
No mandatory statewide residential code; cities and counties adopt the IRC locally, often with amendments.
Pool barriers
No state statute or statewide model code for residential pool barriers; set by local ordinance or the locally adopted IRC/ISPSC.
Contractor licensing
No statewide license for residential pool contractors; licensed locally (electrical work is licensed separately by the state).
Public pools
Wyoming Department of Agriculture, Consumer Health Services, under the Swimming Pool and Spa Health and Safety Act (W.S. 35-28-101 to -110) and the Wyoming Pool Rule, Chapters 1 to 7 (Chapter 2: plan submission).
Engineer licensing
Wyoming Board of Professional Engineers and Professional Land Surveyors

WYOMING QUESTIONS

Questions about Wyoming pool plans.

Is there a statewide residential building code in Wyoming?

No. Wyoming does not require local code adoption. Cities and counties with building departments adopt the IRC locally, often with amendments, so pool drawings must follow that jurisdiction's adopted edition and pool provisions.

Do public pools in Wyoming need state plan approval?

Yes. The Wyoming Department of Agriculture, Consumer Health Services, licenses and inspects public pools and spas under the Swimming Pool and Spa Health and Safety Act. Chapter 2 of the Wyoming Pool Rule sets plan submission requirements before construction.

Are your Wyoming pool plans sealed by a Wyoming-licensed engineer?

Yes. A Professional Engineer licensed in Wyoming reviews the package, takes responsible charge and applies their seal. You receive a submission-ready set for your Wyoming jurisdiction.

How fast can you prepare Wyoming pool drawings?

Residential drawing preparation takes 2 business days after we receive all project inputs. PE review and sealing, and the permit office’s own review, have separate timelines.

Do you work to my city or county’s checklist in Wyoming?

Yes. Send the submittal checklist or the review comments from your permit office. Your quote lists the drawings and calculations that respond to it.
