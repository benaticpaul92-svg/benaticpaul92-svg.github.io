What the quote form sends

The quote form asks for your name and email address. You can also add the project’s city and state, a note about what you need, your company and a target timeline. On state pages, the city and state field starts with the name of that state. You can change it or clear it.

Your note is kept as part of a short message that also names the drawing service the page is about. If you leave the note blank, the message asks for help to confirm the drawing scope.

When you send the form, your browser also sends the path and title of the page you are on, a fixed subject line and a reference code that your browser creates for the request. If the address of the page you are on carries campaign source, medium or name labels, those labels are sent too. The form does not send the rest of the page address, ad-click codes, cookies or anything kept in your browser’s storage. A hidden field that people leave empty helps block automated spam. If that field is filled in, nothing is sent.

Please do not include payment-card details, identity documents or other sensitive personal information.

What happens after you send it

The form sends your request to a Google Apps Script program that runs in our Google account. The program checks the request. If it accepts it, it saves it as a row in a private Google Sheet. The row holds the time it was received, the reference code, a check value worked out from the request, your name, email address, location, company, timeline and message, the page path and title, any campaign labels and the status of the email notice. If the program turns the request down, for example because too many requests came from the same email address, it saves nothing and the page asks you to try again or use email.

After saving the row, the program sends one email notice through Google’s mail service to our public address, with a blind copy to a second inbox. The notice contains the reference code, your details, your message, the page address and any campaign labels. Its reply address is your email address, so we can answer you directly. If the email cannot be sent, the saved request stays in the Google Sheet. If your browser sends the same request again, the program recognizes the reference code and does not send a second email. The form does not send you an automatic reply.

To limit repeated sends, the program keeps a one-way coded version of your email address and a count of your requests in Google’s temporary script storage for up to one hour. Our program does not record your IP address or browser details. Google may process technical information to run these services. See the Google privacy policy.

We use the details you send to reply to your request and to discuss and quote the work.

Email and copy options

If you choose the email draft link under the form, your email app opens a message to our public address. The draft holds the details you typed, the short message described above and the address of the page without campaign labels or other added codes. Opening a draft does not send it. You decide whether to send it. You can also copy your message and send it from any email service. Copying uses your device’s clipboard and sends nothing to us.

After an online request is accepted, the page shows a short reference taken from the reference code, and a link for emailing your files. The link opens a draft in your email app, addressed to our public address, that holds the short reference, the full reference code, your name and the project location from the form. Opening the draft does not send it.

Hosting and page loading

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical information when your browser requests pages. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Our pages load their scripts, styles and images from this site. They do not load fonts, scripts or images from other services. The one exception is the Google Ads tag described below, which loads only if you allow measurement.

When you follow a link from our pages to another website, such as a permit office page, our pages ask your browser to share at most our site’s address, not the full page address. Other websites have their own privacy policies.

Links with a location code

Some links to our pages, such as ad links, can carry a location number in the page address. On some of our pages, a short script reads that number and looks it up in a file on this site. If the number matches a state, your browser moves to our page for that state and keeps the rest of the address. The page can stay blank for up to 1.5 seconds while this happens. The script uses only the page address. It does not read your device’s location, store anything or contact another service. Adding nogeo=1 to the page address turns it off.

Google Ads measurement and your choices

We use the Google Ads tag to measure our advertising only if you select Allow measurement. Before you choose, and if you select Reject optional, this website does not load the Google Ads tag. When you allow measurement, the tag loads from Google on that page and on later pages while your choice stands. It can set Google advertising cookies, keep Google advertising data in this browser’s local storage, and share information about the page, your device and your interaction with our ads with Google. The tag is set up with personalized advertising turned off. We do not use Google Analytics.

When an online request is accepted, the page adds a note with the page path and the reference code to a list kept inside the page. The list is not saved and is gone when you leave the page. If you have allowed measurement, the loaded tag can read that list, and the page records one conversion with Google Ads that carries the reference code. The form code does not send your name, email address or message to Google Ads, and it does not put what you type into page addresses.

Choose Cookie settings in the footer of any page to change or withdraw your choice. Your choice, a version number and the time you made it are saved in this browser’s local storage. This entry only remembers your choice. It is not used for advertising. The site uses a saved choice for 180 days and then asks again. The old entry stays in your browser until a new choice replaces it or you clear this site’s data. If storage is blocked, your choice applies only to the current page. A choice made in one tab also applies in other open tabs of this site.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, measurement stays off, the Allow measurement button is turned off and a choice to reject is saved.

While measurement is off, each page removes Google Ads cookies and Google Ads entries in this browser’s local storage on this site whose names begin with _gcl_ or _gac_, where the page can reach them. If you withdraw consent, a tag already loaded on the current page is updated to denied, and the tag does not load on later pages. A tag already loaded may send limited consent-status information to Google. Information already sent is not recalled. Google’s own data handling is described in its privacy policy and information about business partner data.
